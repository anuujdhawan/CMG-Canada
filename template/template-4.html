How do I know which Canadian pathway fits?

It depends on your goal, background, status, family situation and current program criteria. A consultation can help organise those questions.

Can you help after a refusal?

Yes. The team reviews refusal reasons, GCMS notes and procedural fairness letters, then discusses reapplication, response or referral options where appropriate.

Is the online assessment an official result?

No. Any website preview or calculator is for planning only and does not replace an IRCC decision.
