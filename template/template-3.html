Recovery workflow

When the route needs to be recalculated.

Refusals and procedural fairness files deserve a clear, evidence-led next move.

A refusal is a signal to review.

Officers decide on the file they see. We pull GCMS notes where needed, isolate the stated reasons and build evidence-backed replies, reapplications or appeal paths — so the next submission addresses what went wrong.

Visitor visa refusedTies, funds or purpose — strengthen the narrative.
Study permit refusedStudy plan or finances — fix the gaps.

Clear answers, before you decide

Questions worth asking early.

Use the guides and tools to understand the route, then speak with a licensed RCIC if your history, documents or deadline need a tailored review.

What happens during a free assessment?
You share the basics of your goal and profile so the team can identify possible pathways, immediate gaps and the most useful next step.
Do I need every document before requesting an assessment?
No. Start with the information you have. The assessment can help you understand which documents and facts will matter most before you prepare the full application.
What does the CRS calculator estimate?
It estimates your Express Entry Comprehensive Ranking System score using factors such as age, education, language, work experience and adaptability. It is a planning estimate, not an official IRCC decision.
Does a PNP result guarantee a nomination?
No. Provincial programs have their own criteria, invitations and document requirements. Use a result as a starting point for a more detailed assessment.
What can happen after a refusal or PFL?
The team can review the stated reasons, consider GCMS notes where appropriate and help identify whether a response, reapplication, appeal or another route fits the file.
