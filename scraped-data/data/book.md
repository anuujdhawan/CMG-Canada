## Book a Consultation

Every consultation is with a real person who has worked on real files — not a call centre or a sales desk. Choose the advisor who fits your situation, pick a session length (30, 45, or 60 minutes), and pay securely through Stripe. In the session you'll get a straight read on your eligibility, the options available to you under Express Entry, PNP, work permits, study permits, or sponsorship, and a clear sense of what filing with us would look like.

- **Sanjay Singh Kumar** — Principal Consultant & Founder, RCIC R705959. Senior oversight on complex files and refusals.
- **Vipin Kumar** — Immigration Strategy & Counselling Advisor. Long-term planning and pathway strategy.
- **Amanpreet Kaur** — Licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant. End-to-end application management.

All regulated advice is provided by RCICs in good standing with the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC), and you can verify any licence at [college-ic.ca](https://college-ic.ca/).
