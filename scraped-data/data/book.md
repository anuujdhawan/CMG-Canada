## Book a Consultation

Every consultation follows a defined scope, written next-step summary and clear review process. Choose the advisor who fits your situation, pick a session length (30, 45, or 60 minutes), and pay securely through Stripe. In the session you'll get a straight read on your eligibility, the options available to you under Express Entry, PNP, work permits, study permits, or sponsorship, and a clear sense of what filing with us would look like.

- **Case Strategy Lead** — Complex-file planning, refusal review and written scope confirmation.
- **Operations Lead** — Immigration Strategy & Counselling Advisor. Long-term planning and pathway strategy.
- **Client Experience Lead** — Intake coordination, document requests and next-step communication.

All regulated advice is provided by RCICs in good standing with the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC), and you can verify any licence at [college-ic.ca](https://college-ic.ca/).
