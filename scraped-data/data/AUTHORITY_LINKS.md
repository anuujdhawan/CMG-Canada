# Authority Links Reference (for unique English page rewrites)

Every page rewrite in this folder links out to **official, high-authority
sources** — Government of Canada / IRCC program pages, the College of
Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC), and Job Bank. Outbound links to
these domains support ranking and add trust. Each page embeds the 2–6 links
most relevant to its topic, placed naturally in the body copy (never stuffed).

## How links are used per page

- **Program pages** (Express Entry, PNP, permits, sponsorship): link to the
  matching official IRCC program page + the CICC register + Job Bank where
  relevant.
- **Meta description / hero**: never link — links live in body copy.
- **Anchor text**: descriptive and keyword-relevant ("the official IRCC Express
  Entry page", "verify your consultant on the CICC register"), opened naturally
  in a sentence — e.g. "You can confirm the current CRS cutoffs on IRCC's
  official rounds of invitations page."
- **No-follow policy**: we keep links standard (follow) since they are
  authoritative government sources.

## Writing rules (human touch)

- Write like a knowledgeable consultant explaining to a person, not like a
  brochure: short sentences, second person ("you", "your"), concrete examples,
  plain language.
- Keep every researched keyword (from the page's own title/meta/headings) in
  the rewrite — same placements, natural density.
- Vary sentence length; avoid repeating the same phrase; never copy the source
  sentence structure.
