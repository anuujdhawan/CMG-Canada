## Hero Section

# Free OINP Calculator 2026

Ontario's new Workforce Priority stream - which replaced the province's older OINP streams in June 2026 - scores candidates through an Expression of Interest (EOI) system rather than first-come, first-served. Our free OINP calculator estimates your EOI points under both paths: the Job Offer path (open to any NOC TEER level 0-5, scored on occupation, wage, and Ontario work experience) and the Self-Employed Physician path. Ontario receives more immigrants than any other province, which makes an accurate EOI estimate a genuinely valuable piece of planning - and a provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points to a federal profile.

Our calculators are built and maintained by licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs) who prepare real applications every day. The scoring logic mirrors the published selection criteria used by IRCC and the provinces, so the result is a reliable estimate - not a guess.

Knowing your score is the difference between hoping and planning: it tells you which levers - language retake, credential assessment, job offer, provincial nomination, French - would actually raise your standing, and which programs are realistically open to you.
