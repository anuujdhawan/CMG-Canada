# Free CLB Calculator 2026

Every Canadian immigration program speaks in Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) - even when your test result is expressed as IELTS, CELPIP, PTE, TEF, or TCF bands. Our free CLB calculator converts your scores across all four abilities instantly, so you can see exactly which programs you qualify for. The difference between CLB 7 and CLB 9 is often the difference between a profile that sits in the pool and one that receives an ITA, which is why getting the conversion right - and knowing which test plays to your strengths - matters more than most applicants realize.
