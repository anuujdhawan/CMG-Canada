## What is the PGP?

The Parents and Grandparents Program (PGP) is the federal pathway that lets Canadian citizens and permanent residents sponsor their parents and grandparents for permanent residence. Unlike most family sponsorship streams, the PGP is capped: demand always outstrips the number of available spots, so IRCC runs a random-selection intake process — commonly known as the PGP lottery — to decide who gets to apply.

## Intake Process (Interest to Sponsor)

The journey starts long before the application. IRCC opens a limited window during which potential sponsors submit an "interest to sponsor" form online. Once the window closes, IRCC randomly selects a fixed number of sponsors and emails them an invitation to apply. Invited sponsors then have a hard deadline to submit the complete package. Miss that deadline and your spot is gone for that round — there is no second chance or waitlist.

## 2025 PGP Intake Update

IRCC announces the PGP intake dates, quota, and eligibility each year on Canada.ca. For the most recent cycle, the intake window has opened and closed, and invitations have been issued. We track every announcement so our clients hear about a new intake the day it opens — and we prepare invited sponsors with a ready-to-file application the moment they receive the invite.

## Income Requirements (LICO + 30%)

Sponsoring your parents is a financial commitment, and IRCC holds you to a firm standard. You must meet the minimum necessary income for each of the three most recent tax years, calculated at LICO (Low Income Cut-Off) plus 30%. The amount scales with your family size, including the parents or grandparents you are sponsoring. Proof comes from your CRA Notices of Assessment for all three years — not your best year, all three. IRCC publishes the updated figures each year, so the numbers you meet today may differ from last year's table.

Source: IRCC — [Income required to sponsor parents and grandparents](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1445&top=14)

## Super Visa as an Alternative

The PGP lottery is unpredictable — and that is exactly why the Super Visa exists. While you wait for an invitation, or if this year's draw passed you by, your parents and grandparents can apply for a Super Visa and visit Canada for up to 5 years per entry. It is not permanent residence, but it turns years of waiting into real time together. Many families run both tracks at once: PGP for the long term, Super Visa for the present.

## Processing Time After Invitation

Even after you submit a complete PGP application, patience is part of the program. Processing can stretch over several years depending on the visa office handling the file. IRCC publishes current processing time estimates, and the official Canada.ca page is the reference our consultants check before advising you on timelines. A complete, error-free submission avoids the delays that pile up from document requests.

## Documents Needed

An invited sponsor needs: application forms, proof of relationship to the sponsored parents, proof of status in Canada, and income evidence — the Notices of Assessment for the three tax years. On the parents' side: identity documents, medical exams, and police certificates from every country they have lived in. We build a tailored checklist for every family and review each document before anything goes to IRCC.
