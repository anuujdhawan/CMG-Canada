## Why Bangladeshi Professionals Choose Canada

### Bengali Community in Toronto

Toronto's East York and Scarborough areas have a well-established Bengali-speaking community. Bangladeshi cultural organizations, mosques, and Bengali restaurants make newcomers feel at home from day one.

### IT and Tech Demand

Canadian tech companies and banks are actively hiring software developers, IT analysts, and data engineers. Bangladeshi IT graduates from BUET, NSU, and other universities are competitive in the Canadian job market.

### Transparent PR Pathway

Express Entry provides a clear, measurable pathway to PR. Bangladeshi professionals can calculate their CRS score, take steps to improve it, and plan their application systematically — unlike lottery-based systems.

### Muslim Community and Halal

Canada's multicultural cities have large Muslim communities with mosques and halal food widely available. Mississauga, Scarborough, and Brampton are among the most Muslim-friendly cities in North America.

### Children's Education

Canada's free public education system, ranked among the world's best, represents a major opportunity for Bangladeshi families. Canadian degrees and credentials open doors globally.

### Pathway to Citizenship

After 3 years as a Canadian PR, Bangladeshi immigrants can apply for Canadian citizenship — providing visa-free travel to 180+ countries and permanent security that no work visa can match.

## Top Immigration Programs for Bangladeshi Applicants

### Express Entry - Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)

For Bangladeshi professionals applying from Bangladesh or with no Canadian work experience. Engineers, IT professionals, accountants, and business managers can qualify with 1 year of skilled work, CLB 7 language, and WES ECA.

### Express Entry - Canadian Experience Class (CEC)

Bangladeshi workers already in Canada with 12 months of skilled work experience qualify for CEC — the fastest Express Entry pathway. PGWP holders from Bangladeshi students who studied in Canada are among the most successful CEC applicants.

### Ontario OINP - Workforce Priority Stream

Ontario's new Workforce Priority stream (launched June 2026) targets tech workers and professionals in the GTA with a qualifying Ontario job offer. Bangladeshi IT professionals working for Ontario employers can be nominated, adding 600 CRS points.

### Study Permit → PGWP → CEC

An increasingly popular route. Study at a Canadian university or college → get a PGWP → work 12 months in a skilled occupation → CEC. Atlantic provinces offer more accessible study and employment opportunities. See [IRCC's study permit page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit.html) for requirements.

## Step-by-Step: Bangladesh to Canadian PR

### IELTS Test

Book IELTS General Training. Target CLB 9 (IELTS 7.0 per band). Bengali speakers typically need targeted Speaking and Writing preparation. Allow 3-4 months for preparation and test scheduling.

### WES Educational Credential Assessment

Arrange transcripts from your Bangladeshi university directly to WES. BUET, Dhaka University, NSU, BRAC, and other UGC-recognized institutions are assessable. Allow 60-90 business days.

### Police Certificate from Bangladesh

A police clearance certificate from Bangladesh Police (issued through the Special Branch) is required for PR. Apply through the appropriate police clearance process. Allow 3-6 weeks.
