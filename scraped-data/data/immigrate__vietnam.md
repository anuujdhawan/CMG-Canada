## Why Vietnamese Professionals Choose Canada

### French Language Advantage

Vietnam's French colonial heritage means some Vietnamese applicants carry functional French skills. Proven French proficiency (TEF/TCF) adds 25-50 bonus CRS points in Express Entry - a meaningful edge in competitive draw pools.

### IT Sector Match

Canada's tech hubs in Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary actively recruit Vietnamese software developers, data engineers, and IT managers. Vietnam's strong engineering universities produce graduates who compete well in Canada's job market.

### Vietnamese Community

Canada hosts one of the largest Vietnamese communities outside Vietnam, established since the 1970s. Vancouver's Richmond, Toronto's west end, and Montreal's Côte-des-Neiges all have thriving Vietnamese neighbourhoods with businesses, temples, and cultural events.

### Montreal - Francophone Hub

Vietnamese applicants with French can access Montreal - a vibrant, multicultural city with a lower cost of living than Toronto or Vancouver and strong demand for bilingual workers. Quebec runs its own immigration pathway (QSWP) for French speakers.

### Transparent Merit System

Canada's Express Entry CRS is fully transparent. Vietnamese applicants can calculate their score, see exactly what to improve (language, education, job offer), and plan strategically - no lottery, no agent fees games, no waiting lists.

### Family Reunification

Once PR is secured, Vietnamese Canadians can sponsor spouses, children, and parents. The large existing diaspora means many applicants already have family in Canada to support the initial settlement.

## Top Immigration Programs for Vietnamese Applicants

### Express Entry - Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)

For Vietnamese professionals applying from Vietnam. 1 year of skilled work experience, CLB 7 IELTS, WES ECA for your Vietnamese degree. IT professionals (NOC 21232, 21211), engineers (21311), managers (10011), and accountants (11100) are well-positioned. Category-based draws for STEM and healthcare occupations are particularly relevant. See [IRCC's Express Entry page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html) for current rules.

### Quebec Skilled Worker Program (QSWP)

For Vietnamese applicants with French language skills (NCLC 7+). Quebec runs its own selection system: you first obtain a Certificat de sélection du Québec (CSQ) through the MIFI points grid, then file a federal PR application. Montreal offers excellent opportunities for bilingual Vietnamese professionals.

### Study Permit → PGWP → CEC

An increasingly popular route for younger Vietnamese applicants. Study at a Canadian university → earn a Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) → 12 months of skilled work → Express Entry CEC. This path builds Canadian language fluency and work experience, boosting long-term CRS and integration prospects.

### PNP Employer-Driven Streams

With a Canadian job offer, Vietnamese workers can access PNP employer-driven streams in BC (Tech Pilot), Ontario (new Workforce Priority stream, launched June 2026), and Alberta (AAIP). A PNP nomination adds 600 CRS points - in recent draws, well above every published cutoff regardless of base score.

## The French Language Bonus - A Unique Advantage for Vietnamese Applicants

Vietnam's French colonial history means a portion of Vietnamese professionals have French language ability. If you do, it can meaningfully change your immigration strategy.

### French CRS Bonus Points

French is tested via TEF Canada or TCF Canada; English via IELTS or CELPIP. Combined CLB 9 English + NCLC 7 French earns up to +50 CRS points. Even NCLC 7 French alone adds 25 points - sometimes the exact difference between an ITA and waiting for the next draw.

### Quebec Skilled Worker (QSWP)

- Quebec has its own selection grid separate from Express Entry
- French proficiency is the most heavily weighted factor
- Vietnamese professionals with French can score well on the QSWP grid
- A Certificat de sélection du Québec (CSQ) is issued by MIFI
- Federal PR application follows the CSQ - processing ~12 months
- Montreal has strong IT, finance, and manufacturing job market
- Lower cost of living than Toronto or Vancouver

## Step-by-Step: Vietnam to Canadian PR

### Language Tests

Book IELTS General Training (target 7.0 per band / CLB 9). If you have a French background, also book TEF Canada or TCF Canada. Both sets of scores are valid for 2 years. Achieving CLB 9 English + NCLC 7 French adds +50 CRS points.

### WES Credential Assessment

Obtain a WES (or equivalent) Educational Credential Assessment for your Vietnamese university degree. Transcripts from HUST, VNU, HCMUT, or other institutions must be sent directly to WES. Allow 60-90 business days.

### Police Certificate from Vietnam

A police clearance from the Vietnamese Ministry of Public Security (through the relevant provincial police department) is required for PR. Allow 2-4 weeks. VMC advises on the exact procedures.

### PR Application After ITA or CSQ

After ITA (Express Entry) or CSQ (Quebec), VMC prepares the complete federal PR application within the required timeframe. Medical exams, police certificates, and supporting documents are all coordinated.
