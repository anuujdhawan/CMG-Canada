## Why Saudi Arabia Expats Choose Canada

### Permanent Residency vs Iqama

Your Saudi Iqama is tied to your employer - lose the job or have the visa cancelled and you must leave the country. Canadian PR is permanent and employer-independent. Commonwealth Migration helps Saudi expats secure the long-term stability an Iqama can never provide.

### Oil/Gas → Alberta Connection

Saudi petroleum engineers and energy-sector professionals possess exactly the skills Alberta's oil sands industry values. Many ARAMCO engineers transition directly into Canadian energy companies, and Alberta actively recruits this talent pool.

### Healthcare Demand

Canada's severe nursing and physician shortage means Saudi-experienced healthcare workers are in high demand. Internationally educated nurses from Saudi hospitals are specifically targeted by the Atlantic provinces under AIP.

### Muslim Community

Mississauga, Calgary, and Edmonton are home to large Muslim communities with mosques, halal food, and Islamic schools. Saudi-based Muslim families - typically of Indian, Pakistani, Filipino, or Egyptian nationality - find communities that reflect their values and culture.

### Family Security

Canadian citizenship gives your children a security no Gulf residency visa can match. Public healthcare, free K-12 education, and affordable universities are major draws for Saudi-based families raising children.

### Saudi Savings Advantage

Saudi Arabia has no personal income tax, and many expats have built substantial savings that comfortably exceed IRCC's settlement funds requirements for Canadian PR.

## Top Immigration Programs for Saudi Arabia Expats

### Express Entry - Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)

The primary pathway for Saudi-based skilled workers, and your Saudi experience counts fully. Petroleum engineers, IT professionals, nurses, accountants, and managers with 1 year of skilled work experience and CLB 7 language qualify. See [IRCC's FSW eligibility criteria](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/eligibility/federal-skilled-workers.html) for the official rules.

### Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP)

An excellent option for Saudi-based healthcare workers and other skilled professionals willing to settle in Atlantic Canada. Far less competitive than Ontario or BC. Halifax and Moncton actively recruit internationally trained nurses and health professionals.

### Alberta Immigrant Nominee Program (AINP)

Alberta's oil and gas sector is the most direct connection for Saudi petroleum engineers. An AINP nomination adds 600 CRS points, and Alberta employers actively recruit ARAMCO-experienced engineers for the oil sands and broader energy sector.

### Caregiver Program

Filipino, Indian, and other nationality nurses who have worked in Saudi Arabia can enter Canada through the Caregiver pilots or directly through Express Entry nursing pathways, then build Canadian experience toward PR.

## Saudi Arabia to Canadian PR - Step by Step

### IELTS in Saudi Arabia

IELTS test centres operate in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. Book IELTS General Training and target CLB 9 (7.0 per band) - most English-speaking professionals in Saudi Arabia reach this with modest preparation.

### Home Country ECA (WES)

Get a WES ECA for your degree from your home country (India, Philippines, Pakistan, Egypt, etc.). Transcripts must be sent directly from your university to WES. Allow 60-90 business days.

### Saudi Good Conduct Certificate

Obtain the Certificate of Good Conduct from the Saudi Ministry of Interior (through the Absher portal for residents). It is required for Canadian PR applications. Commonwealth Migration advises on the Saudi clearance process.

### Commonwealth Migration Assessment from Saudi Arabia

Commonwealth Migration conducts video consultations. We calculate your CRS, review your Saudi employment records, and recommend FSW, AIP, or Alberta AINP based on your occupation and goals.

### Create Express Entry Profile

Commonwealth Migration builds your profile with accurate Saudi employment details. ARAMCO and major Saudi employers are well-known - but all employment must still be documented with experience letters.

### Land in Canada and Activate PR

After approval, make a landing trip to activate your Canadian PR status. Most Saudi-based applicants plan their full relocation around this trip. Commonwealth Migration advises on landing logistics.
