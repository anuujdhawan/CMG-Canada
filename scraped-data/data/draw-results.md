## What Does Your CRS Score Mean?

Your Comprehensive Ranking System score determines whether you receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) in a given round — but there is no single "passing" score. General CEC draws have recently hovered around 515-520+, while category-based rounds for French speakers, healthcare, STEM, trades, transport, agriculture, and education have invited candidates with cutoffs as low as the 360s-400s. If your score is below the general draw cutoffs, your fastest route is usually a category-based round that matches your occupation or language, or a provincial nomination worth 600 points. [IRCC's Express Entry page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html) explains how the system works.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **How often does IRCC hold Express Entry draws?** Typically every two weeks, alternating between category-based draws and general/CEC rounds, with occasional additional rounds.
- **What is the current CRS cutoff?** It depends on the category — general draws run 500-530+, category-based draws often 370-495. Check the latest round above.
- **Do I need a job offer to be invited?** No. Since March 2025, IRCC removed CRS points for job offers entirely, and most candidates are invited without one.
- **How long does PR take after an ITA?** You have 60 days to submit a complete application; IRCC targets 6-month processing for Express Entry PR applications.
- **Can I improve my score before the next draw?** Yes — higher language scores, an extra credential, a provincial nomination (+600), or adding a spouse's factors are the common levers.
