## What Are Special Measures?

Special measures (or public policies) are temporary immigration programs or exemptions announced by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) in response to humanitarian crises, conflicts, or other global events. They may offer extended status, work or study permits, or pathways to permanent residence for people from affected countries or regions. Because these programs open, change, and close quickly, up-to-date advice matters — the authoritative source for every measure is [IRCC's website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html).

## How to Check If You Qualify

Each program has specific eligibility (e.g. citizenship, residence, family in Canada). IRCC publishes details for each measure. We can review your situation and explain which programs may apply and what documents you need.
