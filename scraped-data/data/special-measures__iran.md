## Current Program Status

Canada has at times announced public policies or special measures for Iranian nationals, including fee waivers, extended status, or family reunification. Program availability and eligibility change. IRCC publishes current measures on [their website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html). We can explain what is currently available and how it applies to you.

## Immigration Options

Iranian nationals can apply through standard streams: work permits , study permits , family sponsorship , and Express Entry . Special measures may provide additional or expedited options when announced.
