## See exactly where you stand for Canadian PR

Most applicants don't actually know where they stand until they've paid and filed. This free assessment removes the guesswork: answer ten short questions and your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score is calculated live using the same official formula IRCC applies under [Express Entry](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html). You'll see whether your profile would have cleared recent draw cut-offs, which category-based draws could apply to you, and which provincial nominee programs (PNP) are realistic — all without an obligation to proceed.

### What you get

- **Your live CRS score** — calculated with the official IRCC formula, factoring age, education, language, and Canadian experience.
- **Where you'd be invited** — your estimate checked against every recent Express Entry draw minimums.
- **Your fastest path forward** — a ranked, point-by-point improvement plan, from language retakes to PNP nominations worth 600 points.

The result is a starting conversation, not a final answer. Bring it to a free consultation with our licensed RCICs — Case strategy team, Client Experience Lead, or Licensed RCIC — and we'll turn the numbers into a concrete filing strategy.
