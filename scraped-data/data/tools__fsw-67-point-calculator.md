# Free FSW 67-Point Calculator 2026

Before you even enter the Express Entry pool, Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) candidates must score at least 67 of 100 points on the FSW selection grid - six factors covering age, education, work experience, language, arranged employment, and adaptability. Our free FSW 67-point calculator checks your eligibility against that threshold in under three minutes, using the same criteria an IRCC officer applies. Failing the 67-point test is the single most common reason otherwise strong FSW profiles are declared ineligible, so knowing your number before you create a profile is essential.

## How This Tool Works

Our calculators are built and maintained by licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs) who prepare real applications every day. The scoring logic mirrors the published selection criteria used by IRCC and the provinces, so the result is a reliable estimate - not a guess.

### Why Your Estimate Matters

A few points can separate an invitation from another month in the pool. Knowing your score is the difference between hoping and planning: it tells you which levers - language retake, credential assessment, job offer, provincial nomination, French - would actually raise your standing, and which programs are realistically open to you.

### After You Get Your Result

A calculator can tell you where you stand; only a licensed RCIC can tell you what to do about it. Every estimate is followed by a free assessment with our team, where we review your full profile - education, work history, language scores, family situation - and map the strongest pathway to Canadian permanent residence.
