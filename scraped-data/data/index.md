## Choose who we're helping today

Whether you are applying as an individual, with your family, or on behalf of a company, every card below opens the same in-depth guides the rest of the site uses. Employers can skip straight to LMIA, Global Talent Stream, recruitment and compliance (HGT).

### Express Entry & PR

The three federal programs — FSW, CEC and FST — plus CRS strategy and category-based draws. Links through to the EE hub and the dedicated FSW/CEC pages.

### Work permits

PGWP, employer-specific and open permits, spousal permits, restoration, implied status — consistent with our full work permit guide.

### Study permits

SDS and standard study routes, DLI changes, PAL where required, and PGWP planning after graduation.

### Family sponsorship

Spousal and partner files, the PGP lottery when intake opens, dependent children, and refused-sponsorship routes including appeals.

### Visitor, eTA & Super Visa

TRVs, eTA, business visitors, and the parents-and-grandparents Super Visa — temporary entry handled apart from work and study pages.

### Business immigration

Start-up Visa, self-employed pathways, and provincial entrepreneur streams for investors and business operators.

### Restoration & status Urgent

Expired study or work permits, overstays, and regaining status where IRCC allows — often paired with refusal review.

### Refusals & PFL Core focus

GCMS, procedural fairness letters, stronger reapplications, IAD, and judicial review referrals — never generic re-filing.

### PR card & citizenship

PR card renewals, PRTD, residency obligations, and citizenship — including travel documents tied to permanent residence.
