## Which Draw Should You Target?

The right draw depends entirely on your profile. If your CRS sits in the low-to-mid 400s, a provincial nomination — worth 600 CRS points under Express Entry — can turn your profile into a near-certain invitation. If you speak French at a strong level, the dedicated French-language draws have consistently offered some of the lowest cutoffs in 2026. And if your occupation falls into a category-based stream — healthcare, STEM, trades, transport, agriculture, or education — your experience may qualify you for a targeted round even with a modest score. A licensed RCIC can run your profile against every recent draw at once.

## Browse by Province

Each province runs its own scoring system and cadence. BC PNP draws bi-weekly on the SIRS grid, OINP uses the new Ontario Workforce Priority EOI system, AAIP scores on a 0-100 EOI grid, MPNP issues Letters of Advice to Apply, and SINP opens intake windows rather than scored rounds. See the provincial guides below for stream-by-stream details, and cross-check official numbers on [IRCC's Express Entry page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html) and each province's nomination website.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **How often does Canada hold immigration draws?** IRCC typically runs Express Entry rounds every two weeks, alternating between category-based and general draws. Provincial schedules vary — BC PNP bi-weekly, OINP roughly monthly, AAIP quarterly, MPNP bi-weekly, and SINP on intake windows.
- **What is the difference between Express Entry draws and PNP draws?** Express Entry is federal, ranked by the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). PNP draws are provincial, using systems like BC's SIRS (0-120+), Alberta's EOI (0-100), or Manitoba's LAA points. A nomination adds 600 CRS points.
- **Do I need a job offer to qualify for a PNP draw?** It varies by stream. BC Skills Immigration and Ontario's job-offer path generally require a valid offer; Manitoba's Skilled Worker stream accepts candidates with or without one, and Saskatchewan's priority sectors accept qualified applicants without offers.
- **What CRS score do I need?** General CEC draws have recently required roughly 500-535+, while category-based draws (French, healthcare, STEM, trades) have cutoffs in the 370-500 range. Below 470, provincial programs or category draws aligned with your occupation are usually the fastest route.
