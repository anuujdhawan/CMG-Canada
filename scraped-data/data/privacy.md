# Privacy Policy

Commonwealth Migration Canada is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

---

## Page Content

## Collection of Information

We collect information you provide when you contact us, book a consultation, or use our services (e.g. name, email, phone, immigration-related details). We may also collect information from your use of our website (e.g. cookies, analytics) where permitted. Immigration files by nature contain sensitive personal details - your date of birth, passport information, education and employment history - and we treat that information with the care it deserves.

## Use of Information

We use your information to provide immigration consulting services, communicate with you, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we never have.

## Disclosure

We may disclose your information to government authorities (e.g. IRCC, ESDC) when required for your application, or to service providers who assist us (e.g. secure client portal). We require such parties to protect your information to the same standard we hold ourselves to.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our client portal and communications are handled with care to maintain confidentiality.

## Your Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. Contact us at contact form for requests.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page. Last updated: 2025.

Contact us with any privacy questions.
