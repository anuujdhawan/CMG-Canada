## Licensed Recruitment Meets Immigration Expertise

Hiring from abroad requires compliant recruitment and the right immigration steps - and the two are deeply connected. As a licensed recruitment firm in multiple provinces, Commonwealth Migration combines recruitment (sourcing, Job Bank, screening) with RCIC-led LMIA and work permit support, so you get one accountable team for the entire journey.

Whether you need high-skilled tech talent (including Global Talent Stream), trades, healthcare, hospitality, or agriculture, we design recruitment that meets ESDC expectations and supports successful outcomes. Job Bank Canada is the government's [official job board](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/home) and the backbone of LMIA advertising compliance.

At a glance

- Licensed in multiple provinces
- International sourcing & Job Bank
- LMIA & work permit support
- TFWP & ESDC compliant

## What We Offer Employers

End-to-end foreign recruitment and immigration support.

### International candidate sourcing

We source and screen candidates from abroad for your roles - tech, trades, healthcare, hospitality, agriculture, and more.

### Job Bank & recruitment compliance

Job Bank posting and multi-channel recruitment that meets ESDC requirements for LMIA and TFWP.

### Interview & selection support

## Why Work With a Licensed Recruitment Firm?

Compliance and expertise matter when hiring from abroad. A licensed firm understands provincial recruitment legislation, ESDC advertising rules, and immigration pathways - and keeps them in one coherent process. That means fewer delays, fewer refusals, and a candidate who actually shows up ready to work.
