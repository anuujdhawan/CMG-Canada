## Which Situation Describes You?

### My partner is outside Canada

- ✓ Application processed at overseas visa office
- ✓ Partner remains in home country during process
- ✓ Typically faster in some countries
- ✓ No BOWP (bridging permit) available

### My partner is already in Canada

- ✓ Both sponsor and applicant in Canada
- ✓ Spouse can get open work permit (BOWP)
- ✓ Can remain in Canada during processing
- ✓ Must maintain valid status

### We're not legally married

- ✓ Common-law: 12+ months continuous cohabitation
- ✓ Conjugal: genuine relationship, prevented from living together
- ✓ Strong evidence package required
- ✓ Same pathway as married spouse

## Who Qualifies as a Sponsored Partner?

### Legally Married Spouse

- ✓ Valid legal marriage recognized by the country where it took place AND by Canadian law
- ✓ Cannot be a proxy marriage
- ✓ Cannot currently be married to someone else (polygamous marriages not recognized)

### Common-Law Partner

- ✓ Has cohabited in a conjugal relationship for at least 12 continuous months
- ✓ Must provide substantial evidence of cohabitation
- ✓ Evidence: joint lease, bills, bank accounts, correspondence
Evidence package is critical - this is where many applications fail.

### Conjugal Partner

- ✓ In a genuine relationship of at least 1 year
- ✓ Could not cohabit due to exceptional circumstances (immigration barrier, legal, religious)
- ✓ Highest evidence burden of the three categories
Reserved for exceptional circumstances only.

## Building Your Relationship Evidence Package

This is the most critical part of any spousal sponsorship - especially for common-law applicants.

### Financial Ties

Examples: Joint bank accounts, shared expenses, financial support records, insurance as beneficiaries

### Communication Records

Examples: Text messages, emails, video call records, letters, social media messages

### Photos & Social Life

Examples: Photos together over time, social events, family gatherings, travel together

### Official Documents

Examples: Joint lease or mortgage, shared address on IDs, wedding certificates, marriage registration

### Statutory Declarations

Examples: Declarations from family, friends, religious leaders confirming the relationship

### Travel History

Examples: Visits to each other, travel together, entry stamps, tickets and hotel records

We help you build the strongest possible evidence package. A weak evidence package is the #1 cause of spousal sponsorship refusals.

## Inland sponsorship work permit (open work permit)

Inland sponsorship applicants may be able to apply for an open work permit while the PR application is being processed. Eligibility depends on the specific IRCC program rules and current policy.

If your spouse or partner is already in Canada on valid temporary status and you submit an inland spousal sponsorship, an open work permit may allow them to work for any employer while waiting for a decision.

This allows them to work for any employer in Canada while waiting for their PR - maintaining their status and income.

We includes BOWP guidance in every inland sponsorship.

### Eligibility

- ✓ Already in Canada on valid status
- ✓ Inland sponsorship submitted
- ✓ Work permit application submitted at the correct stage
- ✓ Can work for any employer
- ✓ Valid until PR decision
