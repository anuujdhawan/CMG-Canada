## Express Entry & Permanent Residence

### What is Express Entry?

Express Entry is the system used by IRCC to manage applications for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Canadian Experience Class, and Federal Skilled Trades Program. You create a profile, get a CRS score, and may receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) for permanent residence. [IRCC's Express Entry page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html) is the authoritative starting point.

### What is a good CRS score?

Draw cut-offs vary. Recently, scores have ranged from the high 400s to 500+. A provincial nomination adds 600 points. We can help you estimate your CRS and explore ways to improve it — often a few targeted changes move a profile from "waiting" to "invited."

### How long does PR take after ITA?

Most Express Entry applications are processed within about 6 months from the date you submit your complete application after receiving an ITA, though individual files vary with completeness and background checks.

## Work & Study Permits

### What is an LMIA?

A Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) is a document from Employment and Social Development Canada that some employers need before hiring a foreign worker. A positive LMIA can support a work permit application and, in some cases, add CRS points for Express Entry.

### Can I work while on a study permit?

Most study permit holders can work up to 20 hours per week during the academic session and full-time during scheduled breaks. Some programs require a co-op work permit for mandatory work placements. Check current rules on [IRCC's working while studying page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work.html).

### How long is a PGWP valid?

The length of a Post-Graduation Work Permit depends on your program length. Programs of 2 years or more at an eligible DLI often result in a 3-year PGWP.

## Family Sponsorship & Other

### Who can I sponsor?

You may be able to sponsor your spouse or common-law partner, dependent children, parents, and grandparents, subject to eligibility and program requirements. Income and other criteria apply for some categories.

### What if my application was refused?

Refusals can often be addressed by reapplying with a stronger application or by filing an appeal or judicial review where applicable. Our team assists with refusal responses and appeals.

### Do I need to use a Licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant?

You are not required to use a representative. However, a Licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) can help you choose the right program, avoid errors, and improve your chances of success. We are regulated by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC).
