## Why Indian Professionals Choose Canada

Canada has welcomed more immigrants from India than from any other country for several years running. Here is why Indian families keep choosing Canada.

### World's Largest Indian Diaspora Hub

Brampton has the highest percentage of South Asian residents of any major Canadian city. Mississauga, Surrey (BC), Calgary, and Edmonton all have large, established Indian communities with temples, cultural centres, and Indian grocery stores.

### Permanent Residency Path is Clear

Unlike the US H-1B, Canada's Express Entry provides a transparent pathway to PR — no lottery, no employer dependency, no decades-long wait. Indian applicants know exactly what score they need and can take concrete steps to improve it.

### Quality of Life and Universal Healthcare

Canada's publicly funded healthcare system, strong public schools, and social services represent a significant quality-of-life upgrade for Indian families — particularly for those with children or aging parents to bring later.

### Canadian Citizenship in 3 Years

After 3 years of physical presence as a PR, Indian immigrants can apply for Canadian citizenship — including a Canadian passport with visa-free access to 180+ countries.

### IT and Tech Demand

Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary are booming tech hubs. Indian software developers, IT analysts, and engineers are in high demand at Canadian tech companies, startups, and major banks.

### Family Reunification Options

Super Visa for parents (up to 5 years per visit), PGP lottery for parents and grandparents, and spousal sponsorship — Canada has some of the most generous family immigration options among English-speaking countries.

## Top Immigration Programs for Indian Applicants

The right pathway depends on whether you are applying from India, already in Canada, or in the US on an H-1B visa.

### Express Entry - Canadian Experience Class (CEC)

If you have worked in Canada (on a PGWP, work permit, or intra-company transfer), CEC is the most direct route. 12 months of skilled work experience + CLB 7 language qualifies most Indian professionals. Indian tech workers at Canadian companies are among the most successful CEC applicants.

### Express Entry - Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)

For Indian professionals applying from India or without Canadian experience. Requires 1 year of skilled work experience, CLB 7 language, and scoring 67+ on the FSW points grid. ECA from WES required. A competitive CRS profile is needed for FSW draws. See [IRCC's Express Entry overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html) for program rules.

### Ontario OINP - Workforce Priority Stream

Ontario's new Workforce Priority stream (launched June 2026) nominates candidates with a qualifying Ontario job offer at any NOC TEER level, including IT, engineering, and financial services professionals common among Indian workers in the GTA area.

### BC PNP - Skills Immigration (Tech Stream)

British Columbia's Tech stream targets specific IT and tech occupations. Indian software developers and IT professionals in BC can register in the Skills Immigration Registration System (SIRS) for BC nominations that add 600 CRS points.

### Family Sponsorship

The large Indian-Canadian community in Brampton, Mississauga, Surrey, and Calgary means many Indian applicants have immediate family members (spouses, parents, siblings) who are Canadian citizens or PRs eligible to sponsor. Spousal sponsorship processing: 12 months.

H-1B to Canada Pathway

Tens of thousands of Indian tech workers in the US on H-1B visas have moved to Canada in recent years. The process: obtain a Canadian work permit (CUSMA, ICT, or LMIA) → work in Canada 12 months → apply through CEC. VMC specializes in H-1B to Canada transitions for Indian IT professionals.

## Common Challenges for Indian Applicants - and How VMC Helps

### High CRS Competition

India is the #1 source country for Express Entry. The pool has many high-scoring Indian applicants. VMC strategies include targeting category-based draws (STEM, French, healthcare), obtaining PNP nominations, and securing Canadian job offers to boost CRS.

VMC approach: PNP nomination (+600 CRS), French language courses, provincial job offer strategy

### WES ECA Processing Time

WES ECA for Indian degrees takes 60-90 business days. Many Indian universities require transcripts to be sent directly. VMC guides clients through the process to avoid delays.

VMC approach: Start ECA early - at least 4 months before planned Express Entry submission

### IELTS Score Optimization

CLB 9 (IELTS 7.0 in all bands) adds significant CRS points. Many Indian applicants score well in reading/listening but struggle with the speaking band. VMC recommends targeted IELTS preparation.

VMC approach: IELTS Academic vs General comparison; CELPIP as alternative for some applicants

### Police Certificate from India

A police clearance certificate from the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) in New Delhi is required for Indian PR applications. The process can take 4-8 weeks. VMC provides guidance on obtaining the NCRB certificate.

VMC approach: Apply for NCRB certificate through NDML platform; allow 6-8 weeks
