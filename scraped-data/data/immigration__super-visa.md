## The Super Visa - Extended Family Visits

Introduced as an alternative to the PGP, the Super Visa lets parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and PRs visit Canada for extended periods. Whether your parents are applying from India, Pakistan, Philippines, Sri Lanka, or anywhere else - the same rules apply and Commonwealth Migration prepares the full file.

### Up to 5 Years Per Stay

Since 2022, each Super Visa entry allows a stay of up to 5 years - far longer than the standard visitor visa (typically 6 months).

### Valid for 10 Years

The Super Visa itself is valid for up to 10 years (or until passport expiry), allowing multiple entries over a long period.

### For Parents & Grandparents Only

Only parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens or permanent residents qualify. Siblings, aunts, uncles, and other relatives do not qualify for Super Visa.

### Health Insurance Required

You must purchase a minimum $100,000 Canadian health insurance policy valid for at least 1 year from a Canadian insurer before the Super Visa can be issued.

## Who Can Apply for a Super Visa?

Both the applicant (parent/grandparent) and the Canadian host (child/grandchild) must meet eligibility requirements.

### Applicant Requirements

- Parent or grandparent of a Canadian citizen or permanent resident
- Not inadmissible to Canada (criminal, medical)
- Valid passport with sufficient validity
- Genuine intent to leave Canada at end of authorized stay
- $100K+ Canadian health insurance purchased before visa issuance
- Medical exam (if required by IRCC based on country of residence)
- Police certificate if requested by the visa officer

### Host (Sponsor) Requirements

- Canadian citizen or permanent resident
- Must be the child or grandchild of the applicant
- Must meet Minimum Necessary Income (LICO threshold - see below)
- Must sign a letter of invitation confirming full financial support
- Must provide proof of status (citizenship certificate, PR card, or Indian Status document)
- Must list all dependent children and adults living in the household

## Super Visa Document Checklist

Incomplete applications are the most common cause of delays. We review every document before submission.

### Applicant Documents

- Valid passport (6+ months beyond stay)
- Completed application forms
- Two passport-style photographs
- Proof of $100K+ Canadian health insurance
- Proof of funds (if applicable)
- Medical exam results (if required)
- Biometrics

### Host Documents

- Letter of invitation from child/grandchild
- Proof of Canadian citizenship or PR status
- Proof of relationship to applicant
- Proof of income meeting LICO threshold (T4, NOA, pay stubs, employment letter)
- Proof of household size
- Any additional documents requested by the officer
Health Insurance Requirements 2026:

Minimum $100,000 coverage · From a Canadian insurance company · Valid for at least 1 year from date of entry · Must be purchased BEFORE the visa is issued

## How to Apply for a Super Visa

### Confirm Host Meets LICO

Check the current LICO threshold for the host's household size. This is the most common eligibility blocker.

### Purchase Health Insurance

Buy minimum $100K Canadian health insurance BEFORE applying. Get the insurance certificate - you will need it with the application.

### Gather All Documents

Passport, photos, invitation letter, host's income proof, relationship proof, and medical exam (if required).

### Complete Application Online

Submit through IRCC's online portal with all documents. Both parent and host contribute documents to the file.

### Provide Biometrics

Visit a Visa Application Centre (VAC) to provide fingerprints and photograph (if first application or renewal required).

### Receive Super Visa

Processing typically 4-8 weeks. Once approved, the Super Visa is stamped in the passport. Entry can be made within the validity period.

Super Visa Application Fees 2026:

Processing fee: CAD $100 · Biometrics: CAD $85 (if required). These fees are identical to a regular visitor visa application. Government fees are separate from Commonwealth Migration's professional service fees.

### Host's income doesn't meet LICO?

A co-signer (another Canadian child or grandchild of the applicant) can co-sign the invitation letter and combine household income to meet the LICO threshold. Commonwealth Migration advises on the correct documentation.

## Parents & Grandparents Program vs Super Visa

PGP is closed to new applications in 2026. Here's how the two options compare.

### Parents & Grandparents Program (PGP)

PGP grants permanent residence to parents and grandparents. Not accepting new applications in 2026. Past invitees' files continue processing.

- - Result: Permanent Residence
- - Status: Closed to new apps 2026
- - Processing: Multi-year for new files
- - Income: LICO+30% for 3 years
- - Quota: 66,500 total (2026)

### Super Visa (Recommended for 2026)

Super Visa grants temporary status up to 5 years per entry - the practical solution while PGP remains closed.

- - Result: Temporary Status
- - Status: Open - apply anytime
- - Processing: 4-8 weeks
- - Income: LICO for host
- - Multiple entries over 10 years
