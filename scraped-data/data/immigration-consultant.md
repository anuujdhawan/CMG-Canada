## Find Your City

All VMC immigration consultants are Registered Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs) regulated by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC). We serve clients across Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta - with the same licensed expertise whether you are in Toronto, Vancouver, or anywhere in between. The profiles below explain what each city means for your immigration strategy, from local labour markets to the PNP streams that matter most.

### Toronto

Canada's largest city and the nation's immigration hub. Nearly half of Toronto's population was born outside Canada - making it one of the world's most diverse cities. The size of the job market makes Toronto a strong fit for Express Entry candidates and OINP applicants.

### Brampton

VMC's home city - one of Canada's fastest-growing and most diverse municipalities. Brampton's population is over 70% visible minority, with South Asian communities forming the largest group. Clients meet us in person at our Kennedy Road office.

### Mississauga

Canada's sixth largest city and Peel Region's commercial hub. Mississauga's Square One area and financial district attract large numbers of skilled professionals seeking work permits and Express Entry PR.

### Markham

York Region's technology capital - home to IBM, Huawei, and hundreds of tech firms. Markham has one of Canada's largest Chinese-Canadian communities and significant South Asian and Middle Eastern populations.

### Vaughan

One of York Region's fastest-growing cities with a large Italian-Canadian heritage community and growing South Asian and Latin American populations. Vaughan Metropolitan Centre is emerging as a major employment hub.

### Hamilton

A growing city of 600,000+ at the western end of Lake Ontario. Hamilton's healthcare sector (Hamilton Health Sciences), McMaster University, and manufacturing base create strong demand for work permits and Express Entry.

### Ottawa

Canada's capital city with a large federal government, technology, and diplomatic sector. Ottawa has a significant French-speaking population - making French-language Express Entry draws particularly relevant.

### Vancouver

Canada's Pacific gateway and one of the world's most desirable cities. Vancouver's tech industry, film sector, and Asia-Pacific trade connections create massive demand for skilled worker permits and BC PNP nominations.

### Calgary

Alberta's economic engine - Canada's energy capital with a growing tech, finance, and agri-business sector. Calgary has a large and diverse immigrant population with strong demand for LMIA, AAIP nominations, and skilled worker permits.

### Edmonton

Alberta's capital and Canada's gateway to the North. Edmonton's oil sands, healthcare, and education sectors create strong demand for skilled workers, LMIA processing, and AAIP nominations.

Always verify your immigration consultant is licensed.

Only CICC-regulated RCICs are authorized to provide immigration advice for a fee in Canada. Check any consultant at iccrc-crcic.ca before you hire. All VMC consultants are listed there. You can also confirm program rules directly with [IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html) so you never rely on second-hand guidance.
