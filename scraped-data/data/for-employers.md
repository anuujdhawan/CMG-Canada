## Employer Immigration FAQ

Processing times vary by stream. Global Talent Stream targets 2 weeks. Standard high-wage and low-wage streams typically take 2-4 months. Agricultural streams vary seasonally. We provide current ESDC estimates at consultation. You can also compare against [IRCC's employer processing times](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html).

GTS is a fast-track LMIA stream for tech and innovation roles. Category A covers in-demand occupations; Category B covers unique and specialized talent. Processing target is approximately 2 weeks.

You must pay the wages offered, provide the working conditions stated in the LMIA, and maintain records for 6 years. ESDC can inspect at any time. Non-compliance can result in bans and fines. We keep you audit-ready.

High-wage LMIA applies when the wage meets or exceeds the provincial median. Low-wage LMIA applies below that threshold and has additional requirements including a transition plan.

ESDC charges $1,000 per position for most LMIA streams (agricultural streams are exempt). Our professional fees are separate and quoted at consultation.
