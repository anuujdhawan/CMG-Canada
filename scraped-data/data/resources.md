## Immigration Resources

The difference between a smooth application and a stressful one is almost always preparation. These free resources are built by our licensed RCICs from the files they prepare every day - so you know exactly what IRCC expects before you hit submit.

Complete document checklists for Express Entry, work permits, study permits, family sponsorship, and visitor visas. Know exactly what to prepare before you apply - and avoid the missing-document requests that slow down thousands of applications every year.

- Work & study permit docs

Current IRCC processing times for all major immigration streams, updated monthly. - Express Entry & PNP timelines
