## Each Province Has Its Own Scoring System

No two provinces rank candidates the same way, which is why the same profile can be invited in one province and wait in another. Here's the scoring logic behind the main programs:

**BC PNP** — Skills Immigration Ranking System (0-120+). Based on job offer wage, education, language, Canadian experience, and regional bonus (outside Metro Vancouver).

**OINP (Ontario)** — All 8 legacy OINP streams (HCP, Employer Job Offer, Healthcare, French-Speaking, etc.) closed June 26, 2026 and were replaced by the single new Ontario Workforce Priority stream - a unified points-based Expression of Interest (EOI) system, not federal CRS-based. Job-offer applicants (any NOC TEER 0-5) are scored out of 130 points; self-employed physicians (no job offer required) are scored out of 115 (not scored on hourly wage). The stream just launched - Ontario has not published any invitation rounds or cutoff scores yet.

**AAIP (Alberta)** — Alberta Expression of Interest points grid (0-100). Based on education, work experience, language, Alberta job offer, and adaptability factors.

**SINP (Saskatchewan)** — SINP uses intake windows rather than scored draws. 2026 redesign: 3-tier system with priority sectors (open anytime) and capped sector windows (limited positions per window).

**MPNP (Manitoba)** — Letters of Advice to Apply - points (0-1000). Based on connection to Manitoba, employment offer, education, language, age, and adaptability factors.
