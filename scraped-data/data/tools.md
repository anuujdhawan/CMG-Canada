## Free Canadian Immigration Calculators

Your eligibility for Canadian immigration comes down to numbers — your CRS score under Express Entry, your CLB level against the language benchmarks, and whether your profile fits a provincial program like OINP, AAIP, SINP, BC PNP, or MPNP. These free tools put those numbers in your hands in under two minutes, with no sign-up and no email required. They were built and validated by our licensed RCICs, so the logic mirrors how IRCC actually scores profiles.

### What You Can Calculate

- **Express Entry CRS Calculator** — estimate your Comprehensive Ranking System points across age, education, language, Canadian work experience, and additional factors.
- **CLB Converter** — translate your IELTS, CELPIP, or TEF results into Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) levels used across federal and provincial programs.
- **PNP Eligibility Checks** — see whether your profile fits Ontario (OINP), Alberta (AAIP), Saskatchewan (SINP), British Columbia (BC PNP), Manitoba (MPNP), and other provincial nominee streams.

### How to Use These Tools

Pick the calculator that matches your goal, answer the short questions, and review your estimated score instantly. The results are a starting point for your strategy — for an official reading of your pathway, [IRCC's Express Entry eligibility page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/eligibility.html) sets out the rules, and our consultants will map your score to a concrete plan.
