# Canada Immigration Document Checklists

Free, comprehensive checklists for every major Canadian immigration application - so you know exactly what to prepare before you apply.

Missing documents are the #1 reason for IRCC delays and refusals.

These checklists are based on IRCC requirements current as of May 2026. Every application is unique - a licensed RCIC at VMC Immigration Services can review your specific situation before you submit.

## Choose Your Application Type

### Express Entry

Complete document checklist for Express Entry PR (FSW, CEC, FST): mandatory and supporting documents with practical tips from Licensed RCICs. Free guide.

### Work Permit

Complete document checklist for Canadian work permits: employer-specific, LMIA-backed, and LMIA-exempt, with every document explained by Licensed RCICs.

### Study Permit

Complete document checklist for Canadian study permit applications. Includes SDS (Student Direct Stream) and regular stream documents, with licensed RCIC tips.

### Spousal Sponsorship

Complete document checklist for sponsoring a spouse or common-law partner for Canadian PR, inland and outland, with documents explained by Licensed RCICs.

### Visitor Visa

Complete document checklist for a Canadian visitor visa (TRV), with tips on proving ties to your home country, the most common refusal reason. Free guide.

## Why Use a Licensed RCIC Instead of Applying Alone?

### Refusal prevention

Most refusals come from preventable mistakes - wrong documents, inconsistent details, or inadequate ties evidence. An RCIC catches these before submission.

### Faster processing

Complete, well-organized applications process faster. We ensure your application is in order from day one.

### Custom checklist for your case

Every situation is different. The RCIC assigned to you will create a personalized document list based on your specific circumstances.

### Ongoing support

We respond to IRCC queries on your behalf, track your application, and keep you informed throughout the process.
