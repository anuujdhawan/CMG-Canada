## Which Canadian Visitor Document Do You Need?

The right document depends on your nationality, purpose of visit, and how you are traveling to Canada.

### I need a Visitor Visa (TRV)

Citizens of visa-required countries must obtain a Temporary Resident Visa before traveling to Canada by any means of transport. IRCC's official visit-Canada page explains entry requirements.

- ✓ Tourism, family visits, business
- ✓ Valid up to 10 years or passport expiry
- ✓ Single or multiple entry
- ✓ Required for land, air, and sea travel

### My country is visa-exempt (eTA needed)

Citizens of visa-exempt countries flying to Canada need an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA). Quick online process - most approved within minutes.

- ✓ Online application, usually instant
- ✓ Valid 5 years or passport expiry
- ✓ Required for air travel only
- ✓ US citizens are exempt from eTA

### I want my parents to visit long-term

The Super Visa allows parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and PRs to stay up to 5 years per visit - much longer than a standard TRV.

- ✓ Up to 5 years per stay
- ✓ Valid for 10 years
- ✓ $100K+ health insurance required
- ✓ Proof of sponsor income (LICO)

## Temporary Resident Visa - Tourist, Family & Business

A TRV is a sticker in your passport issued by IRCC authorizing you to travel to Canada. The border officer decides your actual length of stay at the port of entry (typically 6 months). Whether you're applying for a visit visa to Canada from Dubai, UAE, Philippines, India, Saudi Arabia (KSA), or Spain - the requirements are the same and Commonwealth Migration handles the full file.

### Eligibility

#### Genuine Temporary Intent

You must demonstrate you will leave Canada at the end of your authorized stay - through employment, family, property, or other strong ties to your home country.

#### Financial Capacity

Sufficient funds for accommodation, food, transport, and return travel for the duration of your stay. Bank statements 3-6 months.

#### No Inadmissibility

No criminal record, no medical concerns, no prior immigration violations or overstays in Canada or other countries.

#### Valid Travel Documents

Valid passport with sufficient validity beyond your intended stay. Two passport-sized photographs meeting IRCC specifications.

#### Clear Purpose of Visit

Tourism, family visit, or business meeting - your stated purpose must be credible and supported by documentation.

### Process

#### Confirm You Need a TRV

Check if your country is on the visa-required list. Dual citizens - your travel document determines if you need a TRV.

#### Gather Documents

Valid passport, photos, proof of funds, travel itinerary, ties to home country, invitation letter (if visiting family).

#### Apply Online

Submit Form IMM 5257 through your IRCC secure account with all supporting documents and application fee.

#### Pay Fees

CAD $100 application fee per person. Family maximum $500. Biometrics $85 per person (if required).

#### Provide Biometrics

Visit a Visa Application Centre (VAC) in your country to provide fingerprints and photograph.

#### Receive Decision

Most decisions in 2-6 weeks for complete applications. You receive a visa stamp in your passport or refusal letter with reasons.

Government fees: CAD $100/person · Family max $500 · Biometrics $85/person · Processing: 2-6 weeks for most countries

## Visitor Record - Extend Your Stay in Canada

Already in Canada and want to stay longer than 6 months? Apply for a Visitor Record before your authorized stay expires.

### Visitor Record Extension

Apply online before your status expires to extend your stay. If you apply before expiry, implied status keeps you legal while IRCC processes your application. We handle the full extension.

### Implied Status

If you applied to extend before your authorized stay expired, you have implied status and may remain in Canada legally while awaiting a decision - but you cannot travel outside Canada on implied status.

### Restoring Visitor Status

Overstayed your authorized period? Status restoration may be possible within 90 days. We assess restoration eligibility and prepares the application.

## Business Visitors and Transit Visas

Short business trips and airport connections have specific requirements depending on your nationality and purpose.

### Business Visitor Visa

For meetings, conferences, negotiations, and short assignments where you are NOT entering the Canadian labour market. You must be paid from outside Canada.

- ✓ No LMIA or work permit required
- ✓ Paid by a foreign employer
- ✓ Activities: meetings, site inspections, training, after-sales service
- ✓ Cannot perform productive Canadian work
Commonwealth Migration advises on the line between business visitor and worker requiring a permit.

### Transit Visa

Passing through a Canadian airport? Some nationalities require a Transit Visa even for short layovers. Check requirements before booking.

- ✓ Required for some nationalities even for airport transit
- ✓ Direct Transit Authorization for 24-hour layovers (eligible countries)
- ✓ Apply before travel - cannot apply at the airport
- ✓ CAD $15 government fee

## Why Visitor Visas Get Refused - and How We Help

Refusals are common and specific. Understanding the reason is the first step to a successful reapplication.

### Weak ties to home country

Reason: Officer not satisfied you will return home. No employment, property, family, or community connections documented.

We fix: Commonwealth Migration builds a compelling ties-to-home package for every application - employment letters, property documents, family ties.

### Insufficient proof of funds

Reason: Bank statements don't show consistent balance or sufficient amounts for the planned trip duration.

We fix: Commonwealth Migration advises on acceptable fund levels, documentation formats, and how to present financial evidence by country.

### Incomplete application

Reason: Missing forms, photos not meeting specs, or supporting documents absent.

We fix: We review every document against IRCC standards before submission - nothing is left missing.

### Previous immigration violations

Reason: Prior overstays, refusals, or misrepresentation in any country's immigration application.

We fix: Commonwealth Migration advises on disclosure strategy and whether a TRP or other remediation is needed before reapplying.
