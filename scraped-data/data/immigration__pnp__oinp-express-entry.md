# OINP Express Entry Streams 2026 - Human Capital Priorities, Skilled Trades and French-Language - Historical Guide

Ontario's Express Entry-aligned streams (Human Capital Priorities, Skilled Trades, and French-Speaking Skilled Worker) operated from 2015 until June 26, 2026, when they were replaced by the Ontario Workforce Priority stream. This page is a historical guide to those former streams and provides guidance on the new replacement stream.

These OINP Streams Ended June 26, 2026

The Human Capital Priorities, French-Speaking Skilled Worker, and Skilled Trades streams were revoked when Ontario Regulation 422/17 came into force on June 26, 2026. Ontario launched a single replacement stream: the Ontario Workforce Priority stream, with three pathways (TEER 0-3, TEER 4-5, and Self-Employed Physicians). The EOI portal is closed and expected to reopen later in summer 2026.

The former OINP Express Entry streams operated until June 26, 2026. They did not require a direct application — Ontario searched the federal Express Entry pool and sent a Notification of Interest (NOI) to eligible candidates. ## Former OINP Express Entry Streams - All Closed June 26, 2026

### Human Capital Priorities (HCP)

This stream closed on June 26, 2026 when Ontario Regulation 422/17 came into force. Historically, Ontario searched the federal Express Entry pool and sent NOIs to qualifying candidates — no job offer required. In 2024, 12,731 HCP NOIs were issued. No new EOIs or applications are being accepted. The replacement stream is the Ontario Workforce Priority stream.

#### Requirements

- Active Express Entry profile (FSW or CEC - not FST)
- NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3 occupation
- Language: CLB 7+ in all 4 skills (TEER 0-2) or CLB 5+ (TEER 3)
- Valid ECA for foreign education (not older than 5 years)
- At least 1 year of skilled work experience (Canadian or foreign)
- Intent to reside in Ontario

### Skilled Trades Stream

This stream was suspended November 14, 2025 citing systemic misrepresentation and fraud; outstanding applications were returned to applicants. It was formally revoked on June 26, 2026. In 2024, 9,006 Skilled Trades NOIs were issued under this stream. No new applications are being accepted.

#### Requirements

- Active Express Entry profile
- NOC TEER 2 or 3 trade occupation
- Language: CLB 5+ in all 4 skills
- At least 1 year of skilled trades experience
- Intent to live and work in Ontario

### French-Speaking Skilled Worker

This stream closed on June 26, 2026. Historically for French-speaking candidates in the Express Entry pool — among the lowest CRS cutoffs of any OINP stream. In 2024, 1,525 French-Speaking NOIs were issued. No new EOIs or applications are being accepted.

#### Requirements

- Active Express Entry profile
- NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3 occupation
- French at CLB 7+ (all 4 skills) - TEF Canada or TCF Canada
- English at CLB 6+ (if applicable)
- Intent to reside in Ontario

## OINP EOI Score vs. Federal CRS Score - Key Difference

Your OINP EOI (Expression of Interest) score is not the same as your federal CRS score. Ontario calculates its own score based on: your job offer location (GTA vs regional), wage level, Ontario work experience, Ontario education, language level, and NOC TEER. A candidate with a CRS of 420 might have an OINP EOI score of 55 — or 70 — depending on these factors.

This is why a candidate who has been waiting in the federal Express Entry pool for 2+ years at CRS 430 might receive an Ontario nomination in 3 months through the right OINP stream.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What replaced the OINP Express Entry streams?

Ontario launched the Ontario Workforce Priority stream on June 26, 2026, replacing all three Express Entry-aligned streams (HCP, Skilled Trades, French-Speaking Skilled Worker) and the employer job offer streams. The Workforce Priority stream has three pathways: TEER 0-3 (job offer + CLB 6 + post-secondary), TEER 4-5 (job offer + CLB 4 + 9 months experience), and Self-Employed Physicians (CPSO + OHIP eligibility, no job offer). The EOI portal is expected to reopen later in summer 2026.

### How did OINP Express Entry work before June 26, 2026?

The former Express Entry-aligned streams did not require a direct application. Ontario searched the federal Express Entry pool and sent a Notification of Interest (NOI) to eligible candidates. Candidates had 45 days to submit an OINP application. If approved, Ontario nominated them and IRCC added +600 CRS points to the Express Entry profile — in recent draws, well above every published cutoff (IRCC sets the cutoff each round).

### What CRS score was needed for OINP HCP in 2026?

The HCP stream is now closed (revoked June 26, 2026). Historically, HCP draws targeted candidates with CRS scores in the 400-480 range, well below the all-program federal draw cutoff. The new Ontario Workforce Priority stream uses its own EOI scoring system, details of which will be confirmed when the portal reopens.

### Why was the OINP Skilled Trades stream revoked?

The OINP Skilled Trades stream was suspended November 14, 2025 citing systemic misrepresentation and fraud; outstanding applications were returned to applicants. It was formally revoked when Ontario Regulation 422/17 came into force on June 26, 2026. Skilled trades workers may qualify under the new Ontario Workforce Priority stream depending on their TEER level and job offer.

### What should I do if I was planning to apply under HCP or Skilled Trades?

Those streams are closed and no longer accepting applications. Get a full eligibility assessment to determine if you qualify under the Ontario Workforce Priority stream — TEER 0-3 (if you have a job offer + CLB 6 + post-secondary), TEER 4-5 (job offer + CLB 4 + 9 months experience), or Self-Employed Physician pathway. The EOI portal is expected to reopen later in summer 2026. Being prepared now means you can apply on day one. For the official record, see [Ontario's OINP updates page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/2026-ontario-immigrant-nominee-program-updates) and [IRCC's Express Entry page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html).
