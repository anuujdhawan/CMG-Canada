## Make Payment

Payments for professional services are processed securely through Stripe. If you've received an invoice by email, simply open the payment link it contains — no account needed. If you haven't received an invoice yet, reach out to our office and our team will send one for your specific service, whether that's an Express Entry filing, PNP application, LMIA, study or work permit, sponsorship, or a consultation session.

Your payment is applied directly to your file, and our case team confirms receipt the same business day.
