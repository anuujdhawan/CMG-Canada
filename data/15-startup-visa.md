### Intro (target: "Start-Up Visa consultant Brampton", "business immigration Ontario")
Business immigration to Canada runs through several distinct programs, each with a different route to permanent residence, different capital requirements, and different timelines. Our Brampton office works with founders, investors and self-employed applicants to determine whether the federal Start-Up Visa, the Self-Employed Persons Program, or a provincial entrepreneur stream fits their business and their timeline — and builds the file around whichever route actually applies.

### H2: Start-Up Visa Program
A federal program for founders with an innovative business idea who secure a letter of support from a designated venture capital fund, angel investor group, or business incubator. Successful applicants and their essential team members can receive permanent residence directly, alongside a temporary work permit to begin operating the business in Canada while the PR application processes.

### H2: Self-Employed Persons Program
For applicants with relevant experience in cultural activities or athletics who intend to be self-employed in Canada, assessed on experience and intent to make a significant contribution to Canadian cultural or athletic life — a distinct route that doesn't require a designated organization's support.

### H2: Provincial entrepreneur streams
Several provinces run their own entrepreneur immigration streams — including Ontario's OINP Entrepreneur Stream — typically requiring a minimum investment and a business plan projecting job creation within that province. These usually begin with a work permit while the business is established, with permanent residence eligibility following once performance conditions are met.

### H2: Why business immigration files need specific strategy
Officers assess business immigration applications on the credibility and viability of the actual business plan — not just capital availability. A generic plan, or one that doesn't match the applicant's demonstrated industry experience, is one of the more common reasons these files stall. We build the plan and supporting evidence around what the specific program's assessment criteria require.

### H2: Our business immigration process
1. **Program fit review** — Start-Up Visa vs. Self-Employed vs. provincial entrepreneur stream.
2. **Designated organization or provincial application coordination** — where applicable.
3. **Business plan and evidence preparation** — tailored to the assessing body's criteria.
4. **Filing and work permit coordination** — through to PR eligibility.

### FAQ
**Do I need a designated organization for the Start-Up Visa?**
Yes — a letter of support from a designated venture capital fund, angel investor group, or business incubator is required, alongside language and settlement fund requirements.

**What's the difference between Start-Up Visa and a provincial entrepreneur stream?**
Start-Up Visa is federal and leads directly to PR; provincial streams typically require investment and job creation within that province, usually starting with a work permit before PR eligibility.

**Can I qualify as self-employed without designated-organization support?**
Yes — the Self-Employed Persons Program is a separate federal route for applicants with relevant cultural or athletic experience, with no designated-organization requirement.
