## PAGE CONTENT

### H1
Free Immigration Tools — Check Your Numbers Before You Pay for Help

### Intro (target: "free CRS calculator Canada", "immigration tools Ontario")
Before retaining a consultant or paying government filing fees, it helps to know where you actually stand. These five tools, built and maintained by our licensed RCIC team in Brampton, give applicants across Canada a free, no-obligation starting point — then point you toward the specific guide or consultation that fits your result.

### H2: What's here
- **[CRS Calculator](/tools/crs-calculator)** — see your Express Entry Comprehensive Ranking System score in seconds.
- **[PNP Eligibility Check](/tools/pnp-eligibility)** — surface which provincial nominee streams your profile may fit, with an Ontario-first view.
- **[NOC / Occupation Finder](/tools/noc-finder)** — find the correct National Occupational Classification code for your work experience.
- **[Document Checklist](/tools/document-checklist)** — a category-specific list of documents most applications require.
- **[Free Assessment](/tools/free-assessment)** — a broader eligibility snapshot across federal, provincial, work, study and visitor routes.

### H2: Why we built these as free tools
Self-serve numbers — a CRS score, a rough PNP fit, the right NOC code — are things every applicant deserves to check before spending money on filing fees or professional help. What a free tool can't tell you is strategy: how to raise a specific score, which stream to prioritize when you qualify for several, or how to build evidence around a prior refusal. That's where a consultation with a licensed RCIC picks up from where the tool leaves off.

### H2: How Brampton & GTA applicants use these tools
These calculators are aligned with the same program criteria our Brampton-based consultants use in paid strategy sessions — so your free result and a future consultation start from the same numbers, not two different assessments.

### H2: Related pages
Not sure which tool applies to you? Start with the [Free Assessment](/tools/free-assessment), or [book a consultation](/book) directly if your situation involves a prior refusal or an employer-sponsored file.

### FAQ
**Are these tools really free?**
Yes — no sign-up is required for the calculators, and an optional email is only needed if you want your results saved or followed up on.

**Do these tools replace a consultation with a licensed RCIC?**
No — they give you a starting number or eligibility snapshot. Strategy — how to improve a score, which stream to prioritize, or how to handle a prior refusal — requires a consultation.

**Which tool should I start with?**
If you're not sure, start with the Free Assessment — it points you to the more specific calculator (CRS, PNP, or NOC) based on your situation.
