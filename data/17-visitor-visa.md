### Intro (target: "visitor visa consultant Brampton", "Super Visa Ontario")
Temporary entry to Canada covers several distinct categories — visitor visas, eTAs, business visitor status, and the Super Visa for parents and grandparents — each with its own eligibility criteria and a different bar for what an officer needs to see. Our Brampton office regularly supports GTA families sponsoring a Super Visa for parents or grandparents, alongside standard visitor visa applications and refusal responses.

### H2: Temporary Resident Visa (TRV)
Required for visa-required nationalities visiting Canada. Officers assess ties to the home country, purpose and duration of the visit, and sufficiency of funds — the same factors most often cited in a refusal, which is why we build the supporting evidence around exactly what the officer needs to see rather than a generic cover letter.

### H2: Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA)
A lighter online screening for visa-exempt nationalities flying to Canada. While most eTAs process quickly, some are flagged for additional review — we assist with resolving flagged applications and any follow-up documentation requested.

### H2: Business visitors
For individuals attending meetings, conferences, or short-term business activities in Canada without entering the Canadian labour market. Distinguishing genuine business visitor activity from work requiring a permit is a common area of confusion we help employers and applicants navigate correctly.

### H2: Super Visa — parents & grandparents
The Super Visa allows parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens or permanent residents to visit for extended periods over a validity of up to 10 years — a longer-stay alternative to a standard visitor visa or the Parents and Grandparents Program (PGP) sponsorship lottery. It requires proof of the relationship, a formal invitation letter, sufficient income from the host, and private Canadian medical insurance meeting minimum coverage requirements.

### H2: Why GTA families come to us for Super Visa applications
Brampton and the wider GTA have one of Canada's largest populations of families sponsoring parents and grandparents for extended visits while awaiting a PGP invitation — or as an alternative to it. Getting the invitation letter, income documentation, and insurance proof right the first time avoids the delay of a refusal-and-reapply cycle for a visit that's often time-sensitive.

### FAQ
**What's the difference between a TRV and an eTA?**
A TRV is a full visa application for visa-required nationalities; an eTA is a lighter online screening for visa-exempt travelers flying to Canada.

**How long can a Super Visa holder stay?**
Super Visa holders can stay for extended periods per entry over a validity of up to 10 years, subject to meeting insurance and other requirements.

**Do I need private medical insurance for a Super Visa?**
Yes — proof of Canadian private medical insurance meeting minimum coverage requirements, valid for at least one year from entry, is required.
