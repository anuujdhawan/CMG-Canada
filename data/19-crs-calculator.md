### Intro (target: "CRS calculator 2026", "free CRS score Canada")
Your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score determines whether you receive an Invitation to Apply for permanent residence through Express Entry. This calculator, maintained by our licensed RCIC team in Brampton, applies IRCC's current CRS grid so you can see where your profile stands in under two minutes — no sign-up required.

[Calculator widget — age, education, language test scores (all 4 abilities), Canadian & foreign work experience, spouse factors, provincial nomination, valid job offer, Canadian education, sibling in Canada]

### H2: What the calculator checks
- **Core human capital factors** — age, education level, first and second official language scores, and Canadian work experience.
- **Spouse or common-law partner factors** — their education, language ability, and Canadian work experience, if they're accompanying you.
- **Skill transferability** — combinations of education and language, or education and work experience, that add additional points.
- **Additional points** — a provincial nomination (600 points), a valid job offer in specific circumstances, Canadian study credentials, French language ability, or a sibling living in Canada.

### H2: What a high CRS score doesn't guarantee
Meeting or exceeding a recent draw's cut-off doesn't guarantee an invitation in the next draw — cut-offs move with pool composition and draw type. It also doesn't confirm program eligibility itself; a strong CRS score with an eligibility gap (a missing ECA, an expired language test) can still block your invitation from converting into a completed application.

### H2: What to do with your result
A low score isn't necessarily a dead end — a provincial nomination, a stronger language test result, or an ECA upgrade can each move the number meaningfully. Our Brampton-based team reviews results like this daily and can tell you which lever is realistic for your specific profile.

### FAQ
**What is a good CRS score in 2026?**
There's no fixed number — it depends on the draw type, with general draws typically requiring higher scores than category-based draws.

**What factors go into the CRS score?**
Age, education, language ability, Canadian work experience, spouse factors, skill transferability, and additional points such as a provincial nomination or valid job offer.

**How accurate is this calculator?**
It applies IRCC's current CRS grid accurately; results depend on accurate inputs, particularly language test conversions and education comparability.
