### H1
How It Works — From Free Assessment to Filing, With a Licensed RCIC at Every Step

### Intro (target: "immigration consultation process Brampton", "how RCIC works Ontario")
Whether you're starting an Express Entry profile, applying for a work or study permit, sponsoring family, or responding to a refusal, every file at our Brampton office follows the same structure: a licensed RCIC assesses eligibility before you pay for anything, builds a specific strategy rather than a generic checklist, and stays accountable for the file through filing and decision.

### H2: Step 1 — Free assessment
Share your background, immigration goal, and any prior refusals or applications. A licensed consultant reviews your eligibility across the programs that actually fit — Express Entry, provincial nominee streams, work or study permits, sponsorship, or refusal response — before recommending a paid next step.

### H2: Step 2 — Strategy consultation
A focused, paid consultation where we map the specific pathway for your situation: which program, what CRS score or eligibility gap needs addressing, what documentation the file will need, and a realistic timeline based on current processing standards.

### H2: Step 3 — Document preparation
We build your file to the evidentiary standard the specific program requires — a study plan that answers what an officer actually checks, a sponsorship file with genuineness evidence organized around IRCC's assessment criteria, or an LMIA recruitment record that matches ESDC's requirements exactly.

### H2: Step 4 — Filing & monitoring
Your application is submitted and tracked, with updates as the file moves — including responses to any request for additional information or documentation from IRCC or ESDC.

### H2: Step 5 — Decision & next steps
On approval, we support the next stage — work permit activation, PR landing, PGWP planning, or the employer's post-approval work permit process. If a file is refused, we move directly into GCMS review and a PFL, reapplication, or appeal strategy rather than treating the file as closed.

### H2: What this looks like for Brampton & GTA clients specifically
In-person consultations are available at our Brampton office at 83 Kennedy Rd S; clients across the GTA, Ontario, and the rest of Canada are supported through the same process virtually, with no difference in the level of review or licensing accountability.

### FAQ
**Is the first assessment really free?**
Yes — the initial eligibility assessment is free and non-obligatory; you only move to a paid strategy consultation if you choose to proceed.

**How long does the whole process take?**
It depends entirely on the program — some work permits process in weeks, while Express Entry-linked PR applications typically take several months from a complete application. We give you a realistic timeline during your strategy consultation.

**Can I do everything virtually, without visiting Brampton?**
Yes — every step from assessment to filing can be completed virtually for clients anywhere in Canada or applying from overseas.
