### H1
Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) Consultant — Ontario-Based, Filing Across All Provinces

### Intro (target: "OINP consultant Brampton", "provincial nominee program Ontario")
Every Canadian province and territory except Quebec and Nunavut runs its own Provincial Nominee Program, and each one nominates candidates against a different labour market need. Our Brampton team compares Ontario's OINP against British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba and Nova Scotia streams side by side, so applicants across the GTA — and candidates applying from outside Canada — choose the stream that matches their occupation, not just the one with the lowest headline score.

### H2: Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP) — our home province
As an Ontario-based firm, we file OINP streams directly, including the Human Capital Priorities Stream, French-Speaking Skilled Worker Stream, Skilled Trades Stream, and Employer Job Offer streams. Applicants working or living in Brampton, Mississauga, and the broader GTA who already hold Ontario work experience often have the strongest OINP profiles.

### H2: PNP options outside Ontario
- **British Columbia PNP (BC PNP)** — Skilled Worker, Express Entry BC, and Tech occupations streams with regular Skills Immigration draws.
- **Alberta AAIP** — Alberta Opportunity Stream, Express Entry Stream, and Rural Renewal Stream.
- **Saskatchewan SINP** — Occupation In-Demand, Express Entry, and International Skilled Worker categories, historically accessible EOI thresholds.
- **Manitoba MPNP** — Skilled Worker in Manitoba and Skilled Worker Overseas streams with strong ties or invitation-based selection.
- **Nova Scotia PNP** — Labour Market Priorities and Demand streams, including Atlantic-specific occupation targeting.

### H2: How PNP stacks with Express Entry
Base (Express Entry-aligned) PNP streams add 600 CRS points on nomination — effectively guaranteeing an invitation to apply for permanent residence in the next round. Non-Express Entry ("base") PNP streams run as a separate paper-based application with their own processing timeline. Choosing correctly between the two affects your total timeline by months, not weeks.

### H2: Why file PNP through a Brampton RCIC
Provincial programs change intake criteria, draw frequency and target occupations several times a year. Our team tracks live changes across all active provincial streams and matches your NOC code, language scores and connection to a province before you commit to one — avoiding a rejected or withdrawn application after months of waiting.

### H2: Our PNP process
1. **Stream-fit assessment** across all active provinces against your occupation and profile.
2. **Expression of Interest (EOI) / application strategy** — sequencing to protect your Express Entry profile where relevant.
3. **Nomination filing** — document preparation and provincial submission.
4. **PR filing** — after nomination, Express Entry ITA processing or provincial paper-based PR application.

### FAQ
**Which province is easiest to get a PNP nomination from in 2026?**
There's no universal "easiest" province — each PNP targets specific labour gaps. Saskatchewan and Manitoba have run EOI draws at lower thresholds than Ontario at times, but eligibility depends on occupation and provincial connection, not province alone.

**Can I apply to more than one PNP at once?**
You can maintain one Express Entry profile while exploring multiple streams, but most provinces require genuine intent to reside there. Sequencing matters — get it reviewed before applying to more than one.

**How many CRS points does a nomination add?**
600 points through the Express Entry-aligned stream, placing you at the top of the pool for the next round.
