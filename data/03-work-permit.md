### Intro (target: "work permit consultant Brampton", "LMIA work permit GTA")
Canadian work permits fall into two broad categories — employer-specific and open — and the rules governing each change depending on your current status, your employer's obligations, and whether an LMIA is required or exempt. Our Brampton office works with applicants already living in the GTA on a work or study permit, as well as candidates applying from outside Canada, to file the correct permit type the first time.

### H2: Types of work permits we handle
- **Employer-specific work permits** — tied to a named employer, position and (usually) location, supported by an LMIA or an LMIA exemption under international agreements or intra-company transfer provisions.
- **Open work permits** — not tied to a single employer; available to spouses of skilled workers or students, post-graduation work permit (PGWP) holders, and certain bridging categories.
- **Spousal open work permits** — for spouses/partners of principal applicants on qualifying work or study permits, or accompanying a permanent residence applicant.
- **LMIA-supported permits** — high-wage, low-wage, agricultural and Global Talent Stream categories; see our [employer LMIA services](/for-employers/lmia) for the employer side of this process.
- **Restoration & implied status** — protecting your ability to keep working while a renewal is in process, or restoring status after an expired permit.

### H2: Applying from inside vs. outside Canada
Applicants already in Canada on valid status can often apply for a new or renewed work permit from within the country and continue working under implied status while it processes. Applicants outside Canada apply through the employer-specific or open permit stream tied to their situation, with processing times that vary significantly by country of application and permit type.

### H2: Why GTA and Peel Region applicants choose a licensed RCIC
Brampton and the wider GTA have a high concentration of employer-specific work permit holders across manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, trucking and skilled trades. Filing errors on the LMIA-exemption code, NOC classification, or implied status timing are among the most common reasons for refusals in these sectors — our team reviews every file against the specific program requirements before submission.

### FAQ
**How long can I stay in Canada while my work permit renewal processes?**
If you apply before your current permit expires, implied status lets you keep working under your existing conditions while IRCC processes the renewal — even past the original expiry date.

**What's the difference between employer-specific and open work permits?**
Employer-specific ties you to one employer, role and location, usually with an LMIA or exemption. Open permits let you work almost anywhere but are limited to specific eligible categories.

**Can I restore an expired work permit?**
Yes, within 90 days of expiry, if you meet eligibility and haven't worked without authorization during the gap. After 90 days, you generally need to leave Canada and reapply.
