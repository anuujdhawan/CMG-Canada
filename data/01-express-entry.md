### Intro (target: "Express Entry consultant Brampton", "CRS score calculator Canada")
If you're comparing Express Entry consultants in Brampton or anywhere in the GTA, the number that decides your file isn't your occupation — it's your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score on the day of the draw. Our licensed team, based at 83 Kennedy Rd S in Brampton, works across the three federal programs — Federal Skilled Worker (FSW), Canadian Experience Class (CEC) and Federal Skilled Trades (FST) — plus the category-based draws IRCC runs for healthcare, trades, French-language ability, agriculture, transport, STEM and education occupations. We don't just calculate your score — we identify what moves it, whether that's a provincial nomination, a second language test, an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) upgrade, or timing your profile around a category you actually qualify for.

### H2: The three federal programs
- **Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)** — a points-grid program for candidates with foreign or Canadian skilled work experience, language ability and education, with no Canadian job experience required.
- **Canadian Experience Class (CEC)** — for candidates already working in Canada on a valid work permit with at least one year of skilled experience, common among Brampton-area workers converting from study or work permits to PR.
- **Federal Skilled Trades (FST)** — for candidates with a valid job offer or certificate of qualification in a skilled trade, relevant to Peel Region's logistics, construction and manufacturing employers.

### H2: CRS score strategy — what we actually optimize
We review core human capital factors (age, education, language, Canadian work experience), spouse factors, skill-transferability combinations, and the additional 600 points available through a provincial nomination. Most applicants who plateau below the cut-off do so on language score or education comparability — the two levers we check first, before recommending any paid step.

### H2: Category-based selection draws
IRCC periodically runs category-based draws for healthcare occupations, STEM, skilled trades, transport, agriculture and agri-food, French-language proficiency, and education. Qualifying for a category changes which pool your profile competes in — it does not guarantee an invitation on its own. We track current draw categories and cut-offs as IRCC updates them.

### FAQ
**What CRS score do I need for Express Entry in 2026?**
Cut-off scores move with every draw and depend on the draw type — general draws typically require a higher score than category-based draws. We check current draw trends against your profile before giving you a number to plan around.

**Can I apply for Express Entry from Brampton if I'm already in Canada on a work permit?**
Yes — if you have at least one year of skilled Canadian work experience, Canadian Experience Class is often the fastest federal route, and we review it alongside any provincial nomination options open to you.

**Does a job offer help my Express Entry application?**
A valid, LMIA-supported job offer can add CRS points in specific circumstances and can support a Federal Skilled Trades application — we assess whether your offer qualifies.

**How long does Express Entry take from profile to PR?**
IRCC's service standard for Express Entry applications is around six months from a complete application after an Invitation to Apply, though processing times shift — we track current standards for you.

**Do I need to visit your Brampton office in person?**
No — consultations are available in person at our Brampton location or virtually for clients anywhere in Canada or applying from overseas.
