### H1
Study Permit Consultant in Brampton — SDS, PAL & PGWP Planning for International Students

### Intro (target: "study permit consultant Brampton", "SDS study permit Canada")
A Canadian study permit application is judged on more than a letter of acceptance — proof of funds, study plan credibility, provincial attestation, and ties to your home country all factor into the officer's decision. Brampton and the GTA host a large international student population studying at Ontario colleges and universities, and our licensed RCIC team prepares study permit files for applicants both applying from abroad and already studying in Canada.

### H2: Study permit routes we handle
- **Student Direct Stream (SDS)** — faster processing for applicants from eligible countries who meet upfront financial and language requirements.
- **Standard study permit stream** — for applicants outside SDS-eligible countries or program types, with a fuller financial documentation package.
- **Provincial/Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL)** — confirming your designated learning institution has a seat allocated under current provincial intake limits.
- **Designated Learning Institution (DLI) transfers** — changing schools or programs while maintaining valid status.
- **Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) planning** — structuring your program choice and study permit conditions so you remain eligible for a PGWP after graduation.

### H2: What makes a study plan credible to an officer
Officers assess whether your chosen program is a logical step given your academic and career history, whether your proof of funds genuinely covers tuition and living costs for the program length, and whether your explanation for choosing Canada — and this specific institution — is consistent and well supported. Generic or templated study plans are one of the most common reasons for refusal.

### H2: Why GTA applicants and current students choose a licensed RCIC
For applicants already enrolled at a GTA institution, status maintenance — enrolment, permit conditions, DLI transfer reporting — protects your PGWP eligibility later. For applicants applying from outside Canada, financial documentation and study plan quality are within your control and are exactly where a licensed consultant adds the most value before submission, not after a refusal.

### H2: Our study permit process
1. **Program and DLI fit check** — SDS eligibility, PAL requirement, and program-to-PGWP alignment.
2. **Financial documentation** — proof of funds structured to meet current thresholds.
3. **Study plan preparation** — a specific, evidence-backed narrative rather than a generic template.
4. **Filing and status maintenance** — submission, and ongoing guidance through DLI changes or permit renewal.

### FAQ
**Do I need a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) for a study permit in 2026?**
Most applicants need a PAL or TAL from their DLI's province or territory alongside their letter of acceptance, though some program levels and categories are exempt — confirm your specific case before applying.

**What is SDS and am I eligible?**
SDS offers faster processing for applicants from specific eligible countries who meet upfront language, tuition deposit and funds requirements. Country eligibility changes periodically.

**Can I switch schools after my study permit is approved?**
Generally yes — report the change through your IRCC account and keep meeting your permit conditions, including remaining enrolled and actively studying.
