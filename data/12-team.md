### Intro (target: "RCIC Brampton", "immigration consultant team Ontario")
Every file at Commonwealth Migration Canada is led by a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant licensed with the CICC — not passed off to unlicensed case handlers. Our team, based at 83 Kennedy Rd S in Brampton, works across Express Entry, provincial nominee programs, work and study permits, family sponsorship, refusals, and employer-side LMIA and recruitment files for clients throughout the GTA and Canada.

### H2: Sanjay Singh Kumar — Principal Consultant
Sanjay Singh Kumar is the Principal Consultant and licensed RCIC leading Commonwealth Migration Canada, with 15 years of team experience across:
- High-stakes immigration and refusal files
- LMIA and employer services (HGT Division)
- Express Entry and PNP strategy
- Ontario PNP (OINP), BC PNP, and Alberta AAIP
- Nova Scotia PNP, Saskatchewan SINP, and Manitoba MPNP
- LMIA refusals, procedural fairness letter (PFL) response, and GCMS analysis

Sanjay communicates directly with clients in English, Punjabi, Hindi and Urdu, and personally reviews the firm's highest-complexity refusal and employer compliance files.

### H2: How our team is structured
Client files move through initial assessment, program-strategy review, document preparation and filing, with a licensed RCIC accountable for the file at every stage — rather than a sales-first intake process that hands off to unlicensed staff after the consultation. Refusal, PFL, and employer compliance files are reviewed personally by our Principal Consultant given the deadline sensitivity and regulatory stakes involved.

### H2: Why that matters for Brampton & GTA clients
Peel Region has, unfortunately, seen its share of unlicensed "ghost consultants" operating without CICC accountability — a risk that disproportionately affects newcomer communities. Every consultation at our firm is with someone whose license is publicly verifiable on the CICC registry, for every service we offer, from a first-time study permit to a multi-province LMIA file.
