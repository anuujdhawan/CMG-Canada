### H1
Free PNP Eligibility Check — Ontario-First, All Provinces Covered

### Intro (target: "PNP eligibility check", "OINP eligibility tool")
Provincial nominee eligibility criteria vary significantly by province and stream — what qualifies for Saskatchewan's SINP won't necessarily fit Ontario's OINP. This free tool, built by our Brampton-based RCIC team, checks your profile against the baseline criteria for OINP streams first, then British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Nova Scotia and the Atlantic Immigration Program.

[Tool widget — occupation/NOC, education, language scores, work experience, job offer status, connection to specific province, Express Entry profile status]

### H2: What the tool checks against
- **Ontario (OINP)** — Human Capital Priorities, Employer Job Offer, French-Speaking Skilled Worker, and international student streams.
- **British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba** — Express Entry-linked and base streams by occupation and connection to the province.
- **Nova Scotia & Atlantic Immigration Program** — employer-driven streams across the Atlantic provinces.

### H2: What "eligible" means here
A result showing you meet a stream's baseline criteria means you meet the minimum published requirements — it doesn't mean an invitation is guaranteed, since several PNP streams use Expression of Interest systems with their own ranking or draw thresholds separate from federal CRS.

### H2: What to do with an eligible result
If you show eligibility for more than one stream, the right next step depends on processing time, your connection to that province, and whether the stream requires a job offer you don't yet have. This is where a consultation adds the most value — turning "eligible for three streams" into "apply to this one, in this order."

### FAQ
**Can I check multiple provinces at once?**
Yes — the tool checks your profile against several provincial streams simultaneously, though you'd typically apply to one province's stream at a time.

**Does this show my chances of an actual nomination?**
It shows baseline eligibility, not your ranking within that province's pool, since some PNP streams run their own point or draw systems.

**Does this cover provinces outside Ontario?**
Yes — OINP is covered in depth, alongside British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Nova Scotia and the Atlantic Immigration Program.
