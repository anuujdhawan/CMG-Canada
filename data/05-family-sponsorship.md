### Intro (target: "spousal sponsorship consultant Brampton", "family sponsorship RCIC Ontario")
Family sponsorship applications are decided as much on the strength of your evidence as on eligibility. Officers assessing spousal, partner and parent/grandparent files are trained to look for genuineness — and gaps in the evidence, not eligibility itself, are the most common reason for a refusal. Our Brampton-based team, serving Peel Region, the GTA and sponsors across Canada, builds sponsorship files around the specific concerns an officer will be checking for.

### H2: Sponsorship categories we handle
- **Spousal and common-law partner sponsorship** — inland or outland applications, including cases with a prior marriage, age gap, or limited in-person time together that need stronger supporting evidence.
- **Parents and Grandparents Program (PGP)** — interest-to-sponsor submissions when the lottery opens, minimum necessary income (MNI) planning, and full application preparation once invited.
- **Dependent children** — sponsorship of biological or adopted dependent children, including de facto dependents in more complex family situations.
- **Refused sponsorship & appeals** — reapplication strategy or Immigration Appeal Division (IAD) appeals where a sponsorship has already been refused.

### H2: What "genuineness" evidence actually means
IRCC looks for a consistent relationship timeline, communication history (calls, messages, visits), shared financial responsibilities where applicable, and social recognition of the relationship by family and community. For parents and grandparents, the focus shifts to the sponsor's income history against the minimum necessary income threshold over the required tax years. Building this evidence correctly the first time avoids months of delay on a refusal-and-reapply cycle.

### FAQ
**How does the PGP lottery work?**
Eligible sponsors submit an interest-to-sponsor form during a limited intake window. IRCC randomly selects sponsors and invites them to submit a full application, with the minimum necessary income assessed at that stage.

**What's the most common reason spousal sponsorships are refused?**
Insufficient evidence of a genuine, ongoing relationship — inconsistent timelines, limited communication history, or weak shared ties. This is usually addressable on reapplication or appeal.

**Can a refused spousal sponsorship be appealed?**
Yes, generally to the Immigration Appeal Division within 30 days of refusal, or through a stronger reapplication depending on the specific reasons.
