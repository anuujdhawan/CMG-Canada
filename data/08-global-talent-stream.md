### H1
Global Talent Stream Consultant in Brampton — Expedited LMIA for Specialized Tech Roles

### Intro (target: "Global Talent Stream consultant Brampton", "expedited work permit tech Canada")
The Global Talent Stream exists because standard LMIA timelines don't work for employers competing for specialized technical talent on a global hiring cycle. It carries a 10-business-day service standard for the LMIA portion and a companion two-week work permit processing target — the fastest LMIA-based route available. Our Brampton-based team works with GTA tech and innovation-sector employers to determine Category A or Category B eligibility and build the Labour Market Benefits Plan ESDC requires alongside the application.

### H2: Category A — unique, specialized talent
For employers referred by one of ESDC's designated partner organizations, hiring for roles that are innovative or specialized but not captured by a standard occupation list. This category depends on a strong case for why the role and the specific candidate are genuinely unique to the business.

### H2: Category B — in-demand technical occupations
Covers a defined list of occupations — largely technology and engineering management roles — that qualify for expedited processing without needing a partner referral, provided the wage offered meets the prevailing wage threshold for the occupation and region.

### H2: The Labour Market Benefits Plan
Every Global Talent Stream employer commits to measurable benefits tied to the hire — job creation, skills and knowledge transfer to Canadians, or investment in training and recruitment. ESDC can review these commitments after approval, so the plan needs to be realistic and specific to the business, not a generic template.

### H2: Why GTA tech employers use the Global Talent Stream
The Greater Toronto Area's technology sector competes directly with U.S. and international employers for specialized talent, where a multi-week or multi-month standard LMIA timeline can mean losing the candidate. Employers based in or hiring into Brampton, Mississauga, and Toronto use GTS specifically to close that timeline gap on roles where the business case for urgency is genuine.

### H2: Our Global Talent Stream process
1. **Category and eligibility determination** — Category A partner referral vs. Category B occupation match.
2. **Labour Market Benefits Plan drafting** — specific, measurable commitments tied to your business.
3. **LMIA and work permit filing** — coordinated submission targeting the expedited service standards.
4. **Post-approval compliance guidance** — meeting your Benefits Plan commitments through the work permit term.

### FAQ
**How fast is Global Talent Stream processing?**
The LMIA portion carries a 10-business-day service standard, compared to several weeks for standard streams, with a companion two-week target on the work permit side.

**What's the difference between Category A and Category B?**
Category A requires a designated partner referral for unique, specialized talent; Category B covers a fixed list of in-demand technical and managerial occupations without needing a referral.

**Do I need a Labour Market Benefits Plan?**
Yes — every GTS employer commits to measurable benefits such as job creation, skills transfer, or training investment, and compliance can be reviewed after approval.
