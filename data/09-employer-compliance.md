### Intro (target: "TFWP compliance Brampton", "ESDC audit Ontario")
Hiring under an LMIA doesn't end when the approval letter arrives — it comes with ongoing recordkeeping and compliance obligations that ESDC can review at any point during and after the work permit term. Our Brampton office helps GTA employers with LMIA-supported workers on staff prepare for inspections before one is triggered, and represents employers through active compliance reviews.

### H2: What ESDC actually checks
Compliance reviews assess whether the job duties, wage, and working conditions match what was stated in the original LMIA application; whether the employer met transition plan or Labour Market Benefits Plan commitments; and whether required records — payroll, recruitment documentation, and proof of genuine job offer terms — are retained and accurate for the required six-year period.

### H2: Common compliance gaps we find
- **Wage drift** — the worker's actual pay falling below the wage stated in the LMIA due to missed increases or misclassified overtime.
- **Job duty mismatch** — day-to-day duties shifting from the original job description without updating the record.
- **Incomplete recordkeeping** — missing payroll records, recruitment documentation, or proof of the advertising conducted before the LMIA was filed.
- **Unmet transition plan commitments** — high-wage stream employers who haven't documented progress on their stated plan to reduce reliance on the TFWP.

### H2: Why Brampton & GTA employers get ahead of this
Peel Region's concentration of LMIA-supported hiring across warehousing, logistics, manufacturing and healthcare means local employers are statistically more likely to see a compliance review over the life of a work permit. A proactive audit-readiness review — before ESDC asks — is significantly less costly than responding to a finding of non-compliance after the fact.

### H2: Our employer compliance process
1. **Recordkeeping audit** — payroll, job duties, and recruitment documentation checked against the original LMIA.
2. **Gap remediation** — correcting wage, duty, or documentation mismatches before they're flagged externally.
3. **Inspection representation** — responding to an active ESDC compliance review or request for information.
4. **Ongoing compliance monitoring** — for employers with multiple LMIA-supported workers across renewal cycles.

### FAQ
**How long do I need to keep TFWP records?**
Generally six years from the first day of employment under the LMIA — payroll, job duties, wages, and recruitment documentation.

**What triggers an ESDC compliance inspection?**
Inspections can be random, tip-based, or triggered by inconsistencies in a new LMIA application, a worker complaint, or prior non-compliance findings.

**What happens if we're found non-compliant?**
Consequences range from a warning and monetary penalty to a program ban and public listing on ESDC's non-compliant employer registry, affecting future LMIA-based hiring.
