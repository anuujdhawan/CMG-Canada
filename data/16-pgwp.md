PGWP Consultant in Brampton — Post-Graduation Work Permit Eligibility & Planning

### Intro (target: "PGWP consultant Brampton", "post graduation work permit Ontario")
The Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) is what turns a Canadian credential into Canadian work experience — and Canadian work experience is what makes Canadian Experience Class one of the fastest routes to permanent residence. Our Brampton office works with international graduates from GTA colleges and universities to confirm PGWP eligibility before graduation, not after a refusal, since eligibility gaps are far easier to fix while still enrolled.

### H2: What determines PGWP eligibility
- **Institution eligibility** — the designated learning institution (DLI) must be PGWP-eligible; not all DLIs qualify.
- **Program length and type** — programs must generally be at least eight months long, at an eligible credential level.
- **Continuous full-time enrolment** — with limited exceptions for authorized leave and final-semester part-time study.
- **Online study limits** — a portion of the program may need to have been completed in Canada, depending on when it was studied and current transitional rules.
- **Application timing** — PGWP applications must generally be submitted within 180 days of receiving final marks confirming program completion.

### H2: How PGWP length is calculated
Permit length is generally tied to the length of the study program, up to a maximum of three years, though shorter programs (eight months to under two years) typically receive a PGWP matching the program length. Confirming this before your final semester lets you plan realistically for the Canadian work experience window you'll actually have.

### H2: PGWP as a pathway, not an endpoint
A PGWP is temporary. What matters for permanent residence is the skilled Canadian work experience gained during that permit — generally at least one year — which can qualify for Canadian Experience Class under Express Entry, or support certain OINP streams for international graduates working in Ontario. Planning your job search and NOC classification during the PGWP window is where most of our post-graduation clients need the most guidance.

### H2: Why GTA graduates work with us
Brampton and the wider GTA host a large concentration of international students at Ontario colleges and universities. Program eligibility, DLI status, and online-study transitional rules have shifted multiple times in recent years — confirming your specific program and enrolment history against current rules avoids a PGWP application that gets refused for a technicality that could have been caught earlier.

### H2: Our PGWP process
1. **Eligibility confirmation** — DLI, program length, and enrolment history reviewed before your final semester.
2. **Application timing strategy** — filing within the 180-day window with a complete document package.
3. **Post-approval planning** — structuring your job search toward Canadian Experience Class or a relevant OINP stream.
4. **Refusal response** — if a PGWP is refused, reviewing the specific eligibility gap and next options.

### FAQ
**How long can a PGWP be?**
Generally tied to your program's length, up to a maximum of three years, depending on program duration and institution eligibility.

**Can I get a PGWP if I studied online or took a leave of absence?**
It depends — the proportion of the program completed online and whether any leave was authorized within permitted limits can affect eligibility and length.

**Does a PGWP guarantee permanent residence?**
No — it's a temporary work permit. It builds the Canadian work experience that can support Canadian Experience Class or certain provincial streams, but PR still requires a separate application.
