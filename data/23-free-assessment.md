### Intro (target: "free immigration assessment Brampton", "free eligibility check Canada")
Before you spend money on a consultation or a government filing fee, it helps to know which pathway actually fits your situation. This free assessment is reviewed by our licensed RCIC team in Brampton against current Express Entry, PNP, work permit, study permit, family sponsorship, and visitor visa criteria — with no obligation to proceed further.

### H2: What the assessment covers
- **Permanent residence routes** — Express Entry program fit and a rough CRS snapshot, plus provincial nominee eligibility.
- **Temporary status** — work permit and study permit category fit based on your current situation.
- **Family-based routes** — spousal, partner, PGP, or dependent-child sponsorship eligibility.
- **Prior refusals** — flagged for review by our refusals team rather than treated as a standard new application.

### H2: What you get back
A summary of which pathways you appear eligible for, any obvious gaps worth addressing before applying, and a recommendation on whether a paid strategy consultation would meaningfully change your outcome — or whether one of our free calculators and guides is enough for your next step.

### H2: Why start here instead of booking directly
Not every situation needs a paid consultation immediately — some are straightforward enough that our free tools and guides answer the immediate question. The free assessment exists so you're not paying for a consultation to find out something a five-minute review could tell you.
