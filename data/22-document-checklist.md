### Intro (target: "immigration document checklist Canada", "Express Entry documents list")
Missing or inconsistent documentation is one of the most common reasons an otherwise eligible application is delayed or refused. This checklist tool, maintained by our Brampton-based RCIC team, breaks down document requirements by application category, so you know what to start gathering before you're under deadline pressure.

[Tool widget — category selector: Express Entry / PNP / Work Permit / Study Permit / Family Sponsorship / Refusal Response, generating a category-specific checklist]

### H2: Categories covered
- **Express Entry & PR** — identity documents, language test results, Educational Credential Assessment, proof of funds, police certificates, medical exam confirmation.
- **PNP applications** — province-specific forms, proof of connection to the province, job offer documentation where required.
- **Work permits** — job offer letter, LMIA (where applicable) or exemption code documentation, proof of qualifications.
- **Study permits** — letter of acceptance, Provincial Attestation Letter, proof of funds, study plan.
- **Family sponsorship** — relationship evidence, sponsor income documentation, forms specific to spousal, PGP, or dependent-child categories.
- **Refusal response** — GCMS notes request, evidence addressing the specific refusal reasons cited.

### H2: What a checklist can't tell you
A checklist tells you what document type is generally required — it can't tell you whether your specific police certificate covers every country you've lived in for the required period, whether your proof of funds shows the necessary continuity, or how to present a complex relationship or employment history. That review is where a consultation with a licensed RCIC matters most.

### H2: Translation & certification requirements
Documents not in English or French generally require certified translation alongside the original, and some categories require additional certification or affidavits — a detail generic checklists often omit and that can delay a submission if caught late.

### FAQ
**Is a generic checklist enough for my application?**
It covers baseline requirements, but individual circumstances — prior refusals, non-standard work history, complex family situations — often need additional or different evidence.

**Do non-English or non-French documents need translation?**
Yes — certified translation is generally required alongside the original document, with additional certification in some cases.

**How early should I start gathering documents?**
As early as possible — items like police certificates or an ECA can take weeks or months, and starting early prevents delaying an otherwise complete file.
