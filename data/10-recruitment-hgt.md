### H1
Licensed Foreign Worker Recruitment Agency in Brampton — Domestic & International Sourcing

### Intro (target: "foreign worker recruitment agency Brampton", "international recruitment Canada")
Finding the right candidate and getting the paperwork approved are two different problems, and most employers only get help with the second one. Our HGT recruitment division, operating out of Brampton, is a licensed recruitment agency with authority to recruit for Canadian employers both domestically and internationally — sourcing and screening candidates before a single immigration form is filed, so the LMIA and work permit process starts with a candidate who actually fits the role.

### H2: What our recruitment division does
- **International sourcing** — identifying and screening candidates abroad for roles employers can't fill domestically, coordinated with the LMIA process where required.
- **Domestic sourcing** — connecting employers with candidates already in Canada on valid work or study permits, including PGWP holders and open work permit holders looking for employer-specific opportunities.
- **Employer-side provincial nominee representation** — supporting employers through OINP's Employer Job Offer stream, Saskatchewan's SINP, the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP), and Rural Community Immigration Class (RCIP) employer designations.
- **End-to-end coordination** — recruitment, LMIA filing, and work permit application handled as one continuous file rather than separate vendors.

### H2: Why employers separate recruitment from paperwork — and why we don't
A candidate who's a poor fit but has a technically correct LMIA still costs the business in turnover, retraining and a second recruitment cycle. Because our recruitment and immigration teams work from the same file, the job description, wage, and duties used for recruitment match exactly what's filed with ESDC — closing the "job duty mismatch" gap that causes many compliance issues down the line.

### H2: Sectors we recruit for in the GTA
Warehousing and logistics, manufacturing, healthcare and personal support, skilled trades, and hospitality — the sectors with the highest concentration of LMIA-supported hiring in Peel Region and the wider GTA, where employers are competing for the same limited domestic labour pool.

### H2: Our recruitment process
1. **Role and market scoping** — realistic assessment of domestic vs. international sourcing based on the occupation and region.
2. **Candidate sourcing and screening** — domestic and/or international pipeline built to the role's actual requirements.
3. **LMIA and provincial nominee coordination** — recruitment record and job description aligned with the immigration filing.
4. **Work permit and onboarding support** — through to the candidate's arrival and start date.

### FAQ
**What's the difference between recruitment and an LMIA application?**
Recruitment is sourcing and screening candidates; the LMIA is the separate regulatory application authorizing the hire once a labour shortage is demonstrated. We offer both, and they can be engaged separately.

**Which provinces can you recruit for?**
Our authority spans multiple provinces including Ontario, with employer-side provincial nominee representation in OINP, Saskatchewan's SINP, and the Atlantic Immigration Program, alongside nationwide LMIA-based recruitment.

**Do you recruit candidates already in Canada, or only overseas?**
Both — candidates on valid work or study permits in Canada, and candidates overseas who require a new work permit tied to the role.
