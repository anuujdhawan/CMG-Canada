### Intro (target: "immigration consultancy Brampton", "licensed RCIC firm Ontario")
Commonwealth Migration Canada is a CICC-regulated immigration consultancy operating from 83 Kennedy Rd S in Brampton, Ontario. We work with individuals, families and employers across Peel Region, the Greater Toronto Area and Canada as a whole — on Express Entry, every active provincial nomination stream, work and study permits, family sponsorship, refusals, and employer-side LMIA and recruitment services. Every file is reviewed and signed off by a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant licensed with the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC), license number R705959.

### H2: Why we're based in Brampton
Peel Region has one of the largest and most diverse immigrant communities in Canada, with deep South Asian, Caribbean and African diaspora populations navigating everything from Express Entry to spousal sponsorship to employer-sponsored work permits. Being physically based in Brampton — not a satellite office of a Toronto or overseas firm — means our team understands the specific mix of federal programs, Ontario's OINP streams, and the local labour market that shapes most of the files we handle.

### H2: What CICC regulation means for you
Only Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants, lawyers, and Quebec notaries are legally authorized to represent clients before IRCC for a fee. Working with an unregulated "consultant" or agent carries real risk — from bad advice to outright fraud, with no regulatory body to hold them accountable. Our license (R705959) is publicly verifiable on the CICC's registry, and every consultation, application, and piece of advice is delivered under that regulatory accountability.

### H2: How we work across Canada, not just Brampton
While our office anchors us in Brampton and the GTA, our licensed team files Express Entry, provincial nominee, work permit, study permit, sponsorship and refusal applications for clients anywhere in Canada or applying from overseas. Consultations are available in person at our Brampton office or virtually — the same licensed review applies either way.

### H2: Our specialization areas
- Express Entry and CRS strategy
- Provincial Nominee Programs, with a primary focus on Ontario's OINP
- Work permits, including LMIA-supported and open categories
- Study permits and PGWP planning
- Family sponsorship — spousal, PGP, and dependent children
- Refusals, procedural fairness responses, and appeals
- Employer services — LMIA (all streams), Global Talent Stream, compliance, and recruitment
