## Hero Section

# Misrepresentation to IRCC: answer the record before the ban starts

Misrepresentation can involve a false statement, omission or document issue that affects a decision. The response must identify what was submitted, what was true, what mattered and whether procedural fairness was provided.

- eligibility and admissibility
- documents and timing
- a clear next step

## Page Content

## Start with misrepresentation Canada immigration and the deadline



Collect the application, forms, translations, correspondence, refusal or PFL and a dated explanation. Do not submit a new story without understanding the original record and the response deadline.



- save the complete decision
- record when it was received
- identify the decision-maker

## Build the response around the record



Collect the forms, documents, correspondence, translations, status history and evidence that the decision-maker saw. Explain the important fact in a dated, truthful and document-supported sequence.



- separate facts from assumptions
- answer the stated concern
- do not submit contradictory versions

## Use the correct professional boundary



A finding can create a five-year inadmissibility consequence, subject to the facts and decision. Get urgent advice before a PFL deadline or new application is submitted. Where a matter involves criminal law, Federal Court litigation, removal or another legal specialty, obtain advice from the appropriate lawyer as well as immigration support.



- protect the response deadline
- do not travel on an assumption
- keep every submission copy

## Clarity is a trust signal



Clear scope, current sources, realistic limits and an understandable evidence plan are more useful than a broad promise of success.



- name the decision
- collect the dates
- protect the deadline

## Official sources and responsible advice



For the current rule, review the [official IRCC immigration hub](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada.html) and compare it with the [Canada Border Services Agency](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/). When the issue is personal, urgent or legally complex, ask an authorized representative or lawyer to review the complete record.



- verify current requirements
- keep a complete file copy
- ask who is authorized to help

## Questions people ask



### What is the first thing to verify about misrepresentation Canada immigration?

Verify that the current program or process is open and that your status, documents, timing and core eligibility fit the official instructions.

### Can a calculator or article guarantee an immigration result?

No. Tools and guides help you prepare; IRCC, a province, the IRB or the Federal Court decides according to the process and record before it.
