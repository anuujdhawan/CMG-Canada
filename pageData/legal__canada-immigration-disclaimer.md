Commonwealth Migration Group Inc. does not guarantee visa approval or any other immigration outcome.

Browsing this website, using its free tools (such as calculators or eligibility checkers), or submitting a general inquiry does not create a professional client relationship with CMG or any RCIC. A client relationship, and the professional obligations that come with it, begins only once a Retainer Agreement has been signed by both parties.

## Program Information May Change

Immigration programs, eligibility criteria, processing times, fees, and forms are set by government authorities and are subject to change without notice. While we aim to keep information on this site current, we cannot guarantee that every page reflects the most recent rules. Always verify time-sensitive details — dates, fees, scores, and program status — directly against the official IRCC or relevant regulator website before relying on them.

## Free Tools and Calculators

Tools such as the CRS calculator, PNP eligibility checker, and NOC finder provide planning estimates only. They use simplified assumptions and do not replace an official eligibility assessment. Your actual score, eligibility, or processing outcome is determined solely by IRCC or the relevant authority at the time of application.
