## Hero Section

# Criminal inadmissibility to Canada: choose the remedy before travel

A criminal record can affect entry, a visa, a work permit or permanent residence. The offence, sentence, completion date, Canadian equivalency and purpose of travel determine the route.

- eligibility and admissibility
- documents and timing
- a clear next step

## Page Content

## Start with criminal inadmissibility Canada and the deadline



Start with the complete court disposition, sentence, police records, passport history and rehabilitation evidence. A criminal-record label alone is not enough to decide whether rehabilitation or a TRP is available.



- save the complete decision
- record when it was received
- identify the decision-maker

## Build the response around the record



Collect the forms, documents, correspondence, translations, status history and evidence that the decision-maker saw. Explain the important fact in a dated, truthful and document-supported sequence.



- separate facts from assumptions
- answer the stated concern
- do not submit contradictory versions

## Use the correct professional boundary



No. A TRP can provide temporary permission to enter despite inadmissibility, while rehabilitation may address future admissibility in defined cases. The correct option depends on the legal record and the reason for travel. Where a matter involves criminal law, Federal Court litigation, removal or another legal specialty, obtain advice from the appropriate lawyer as well as immigration support.



- protect the response deadline
- do not travel on an assumption
- keep every submission copy

## Clarity is a trust signal



Clear scope, current sources, realistic limits and an understandable evidence plan are more useful than a broad promise of success.



- name the decision
- collect the dates
- protect the deadline

## Official sources and responsible advice



For the current rule, review the [Canada Border Services Agency](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/) and compare it with the [IRCC: visit Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada.html). When the issue is personal, urgent or legally complex, ask an authorized representative or lawyer to review the complete record.



- verify current requirements
- keep a complete file copy
- ask who is authorized to help

## Questions people ask



### What is the first thing to verify about criminal inadmissibility Canada?

Verify that the current program or process is open and that your status, documents, timing and core eligibility fit the official instructions.

### Can a calculator or article guarantee an immigration result?

No. Tools and guides help you prepare; IRCC, a province, the IRB or the Federal Court decides according to the process and record before it.

### When should I get a file-specific review?

No. A TRP can provide temporary permission to enter despite inadmissibility, while rehabilitation may address future admissibility in defined cases. The correct option depends on the legal record and the reason for travel.
