## Hero Section

# Judicial review: a Federal Court pathway after an immigration decision

Judicial review asks the Federal Court to assess the legality and reasonableness of a decision. It is not a second merits application and strict deadlines apply.

- eligibility and admissibility
- documents and timing
- a clear next step

## Page Content

## Start with immigration judicial review Canada and the deadline



Save the decision, application, correspondence, GCMS or record, and date received. Speak with an immigration lawyer promptly; an RCIC can help with immigration-file context but cannot act as Federal Court counsel.



- save the complete decision
- record when it was received
- identify the decision-maker

## Build the response around the record



Collect the forms, documents, correspondence, translations, status history and evidence that the decision-maker saw. Explain the important fact in a dated, truthful and document-supported sequence.



- separate facts from assumptions
- answer the stated concern
- do not submit contradictory versions

## Use the correct professional boundary



Judicial review is generally based on the record before the decision-maker, subject to limited exceptions. It is not a normal appeal with a new evidence package. Where a matter involves criminal law, Federal Court litigation, removal or another legal specialty, obtain advice from the appropriate lawyer as well as immigration support.



- protect the response deadline
- do not travel on an assumption
- keep every submission copy

## Clarity is a trust signal



Clear scope, current sources, realistic limits and an understandable evidence plan are more useful than a broad promise of success.



- name the decision
- collect the dates
- protect the deadline

## Official sources and responsible advice



For the current rule, review the [Federal Court of Canada](https://www.fct-cf.gc.ca/en/home) and compare it with the [official IRCC immigration hub](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada.html). When the issue is personal, urgent or legally complex, ask an authorized representative or lawyer to review the complete record.



- verify current requirements
- keep a complete file copy
- ask who is authorized to help

## Questions people ask



### What is the first thing to verify about immigration judicial review Canada?

Verify that the current program or process is open and that your status, documents, timing and core eligibility fit the official instructions.

### Can a calculator or article guarantee an immigration result?

No. Tools and guides help you prepare; IRCC, a province, the IRB or the Federal Court decides according to the process and record before it.

### When should I get a file-specific review?
