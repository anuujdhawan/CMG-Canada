## Page Content

## employer immigration services Canada: the employer decision before the form



Fix the vacancy, wage, location, duties, start date, candidate, recruitment history and immigration basis first. Workforce planning through post-arrival compliance.



- classify LMIA or exemption
- confirm wage and duties
- separate employer and worker steps

## A record another reviewer can follow



Keep business, recruitment, payroll, corporate, offer and worker documents in one chronology. Preserve the records required by the current Government of Canada and provincial process.



- keep recruitment evidence
- document changes
- use transparent worker communications

## Compliance continues after approval



Track the permit, wage, duties, worksite, employer commitments, inspections, requests and expiry dates. Immigration support is not complete simply because the worker has arrived.



- calendar every deadline
- store records securely
- escalate issues early

## Avoid three costly assumptions



A job offer is not a permit, an LMIA is not a worker approval, and an exemption is not a waiver of employer compliance. Classify the route before recruiting or promising a start date.



- price the complete process
- document the vacancy
- keep worker communications accurate

## Start with official employer guidance



For the current rule, review the [IRCC: hire a foreign worker](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/hire-foreign-worker.html) and compare it with the [IRCC: work permit requirements](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit.html). Keep the employer file and worker file coordinated, but do not merge their separate legal responsibilities.



- confirm the current stream
- preserve recruitment records
- calendar inspection and expiry risks

## Questions people ask



### What is the first thing to verify about employer immigration services Canada?

Verify that the current program or process is open and that your status, documents, timing and core eligibility fit the official instructions.

### Can a calculator or article guarantee an immigration result?

No. Tools and guides help you prepare; IRCC, a province, the IRB or the Federal Court decides according to the process and record before it.

### When should I get a file-specific review?

Start by classifying the role and immigration basis, then confirm the live program criteria and evidence before advertising, promising a permit or submitting an employer form.
