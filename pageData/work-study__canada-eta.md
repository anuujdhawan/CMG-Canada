## Hero Section

# Canada eTA: a clearer plan for the evidence

An eTA is a travel authorization for eligible visa-exempt travellers; it is not a work permit, study permit or border-entry guarantee. This page is a research starting point; use the linked official source for current criteria, forms, fees, intake and processing information.

- applicants comparing this route with a federal or provincial alternative
- temporary residents protecting status while planning the next step
- families or employers who need an evidence-led route review

## Page Content

## The evidence behind the decision



Start with the dates and documents that prove the claim. Work letters should describe duties; education should be verified; funds should have a traceable source; family and travel history should be consistent.



- applicants comparing this route with a federal or provincial alternative
- temporary residents protecting status while planning the next step
- families or employers who need an evidence-led route review

## What to prepare before a submission



Start with status, dates, work duties, education, language, funds, family, travel and admissibility. Match each claim to the document that would prove it.



- check names and dates across documents
- explain gaps instead of hiding them
- verify the live official instructions

## A direct answer for applicants



The official program page controls current eligibility. A licensed representative can compare the criteria with your facts, but no professional can guarantee a draw, nomination, permit or approval.



- review the current program page
- keep a copy of the submitted record
- track requests and expiry dates

## Common mistakes to remove early



The most expensive mistakes are often ordinary: an expired language test, a job letter that lists a title but no duties, an unexplained status gap, an unverified translation, or a form answer that conflicts with an older application.



- write the timeline
- label the supporting evidence
- ask what fact would change the route

## Questions people ask



### What is the first thing to verify about Canada eTA?

Verify that the current program or process is open and that your status, documents, timing and core eligibility fit the official instructions.

### Can a calculator or article guarantee an immigration result?

No. Tools and guides help you prepare; IRCC, a province, the IRB or the Federal Court decides according to the process and record before it.
